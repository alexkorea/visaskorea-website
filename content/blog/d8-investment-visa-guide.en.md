## What is a D-8 Corporate Investment Visa?

The D-8 visa allows foreigners to invest in Korea under the Foreign Investment Promotion Act and engage in management, administration, or specialized technical activities at the invested company.

A minimum investment of **KRW 100 million (approx. USD 80,000)** is required, and holders can serve as CEO or executive of the invested company.

## Eligibility

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| Minimum Investment | KRW 100M+ (approx. USD 80,000) |
| Equity Ratio | 10%+ of voting shares |
| Business Premises | Registered office address in Korea required |
| Representative | Both foreigners and Korean nationals eligible |

## Application Process

1. **Investment Planning** — Select business and determine investment scale
2. **Company Establishment** — Set up Korean corporation and register
3. **Foreign Investment Report** — Report through KOTRA or foreign exchange bank
4. **Fund Transfer** — Transfer investment funds from overseas
5. **Visa Application** — Apply at immigration office
6. **Visa Issuance** — Issued after review (approx. 2-4 weeks)

## FAQ

**Q: Can I sponsor my family with a D-8 visa?**

Yes, your spouse and minor children can apply for an F-3 dependent visa.

**Q: Can I convert D-8 to permanent residency (F-5)?**

Yes, you can apply for F-5 permanent residency once you meet the requirements.
