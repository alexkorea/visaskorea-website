## Overview of E-7 Visa Renewal

The E-7 (Special Activities) visa must be renewed before the expiration date through the local Immigration Office. Renewal requires proof of continued employment and salary compliance.

## Application Timing

Apply from 4 months before expiry. Late applications after expiry incur fines.

## Required Documents

| # | Document |
|---|----------|
| 1 | Extension application form |
| 2 | Passport and ARC |
| 3 | Updated employment contract |
| 4 | Previous year income certificate |
| 5 | Business registration certificate |
| 6 | Employment insurance certificate |

## Salary Requirement

Salary must remain at least 80% of the previous year's average level. Below the minimum employment support threshold may result in denial.

## Changing Employers

If changing employers, you must obtain a workplace change permit before starting work at the new employer. Working without this permit is a visa violation.
