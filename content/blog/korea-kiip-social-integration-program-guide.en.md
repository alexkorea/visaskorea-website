# Korea KIIP Social Integration Program Guide (2026): How to Apply & Benefits

The Korea Immigration & Integration Program (KIIP) is a government-operated education program for foreigners in Korea, covering Korean language (stages 0–4) and Korean society understanding (stage 5). Completing KIIP provides significant benefits for F-5 permanent residency and naturalization applicants, including written exam waivers.

## 1. What Is KIIP? {#section-1}

The Korea Immigration & Integration Program (KIIP) is a Ministry of Justice initiative designed to help immigrants adapt to and thrive in Korean society.

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Operator | Ministry of Justice, Immigration and Foreign Policy Bureau |
| Content | Korean language (stages 0–4) + Korean society understanding (stage 5) |
| Total hours | Up to 415 hours depending on entry stage |
| Official site | socinet.go.kr |
| Purpose | Support early settlement and self-sufficiency for immigrants |

### Key Features

- Free or very low cost
- 300+ accredited institutions nationwide
- Both online and in-person classes available
- Linked to permanent residency and naturalization benefits

## 2. Eligibility {#section-2}

| Applicant | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Legally resident foreigners | Valid residence status |
| Marriage immigrants | F-6 visa holders (priority support) |
| Overseas Koreans | F-4 visa holders |
| Foreign workers | E-9, H-2, and other employment visa holders |
| Other long-term residents | D, E, and F visa categories |

> Short-term visitors (C-3) and undocumented foreigners are not eligible.

## 3. Program Stages and Curriculum {#section-3}

| Stage | Level | Hours | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stage 0 | Basic Korean | 15 hrs | Korean alphabet, basic greetings, daily expressions |
| Stage 1 | Elementary 1 | 100 hrs | Basic daily communication |
| Stage 2 | Elementary 2 | 100 hrs | Foundational social communication |
| Stage 3 | Intermediate 1 | 100 hrs | Workplace and social communication |
| Stage 4 | Intermediate 2 | 100 hrs | Communication across diverse topics |
| Stage 5 | Korean Society | 50 hrs (standard) / 70 hrs (advanced) | History, politics, economy, culture, law |

> Naturalization applicants must complete stage 5 advanced (70 hours).

## 4. Placement Test and Stage Assignment {#section-4}

All applicants take an online placement test at socinet.go.kr to determine their entry stage.

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Format | Online test (reading, listening, writing) |
| Result | Automatic stage assignment based on score |
| No Korean knowledge | Assigned to stage 0 without testing |

### TOPIK-Based Stage Bypass

| TOPIK Level | Stage Entry |
|---|---|
| TOPIK Level 1 | Enter from stage 1 or above |
| TOPIK Level 2 | Enter from stage 2 or above |
| TOPIK Level 3 | Enter from stage 3 or above |
| TOPIK Level 4 | Enter from stage 4 or above |
| TOPIK Level 5–6 | Stage 5 (Korean society) only |

## 5. How to Apply {#section-5}

### STEP 1: Register at socinet.go.kr

Sign up using your alien registration number on the official KIIP portal.

### STEP 2: Take the Placement Test

Apply for and complete the online placement test. Testing is available year-round.

### STEP 3: Check Your Assigned Stage

Your stage is automatically assigned based on the placement test result.

### STEP 4: Select a Class

Choose an institution near your residence and a schedule that works for you from the list of accredited providers.

### STEP 5: Complete the Course

Attend classes and pass stage-end assessments to advance to the next level.

### STEP 6: Receive Your Certificate

Upon completing all required stages, download your certificate of completion from socinet.go.kr.

## 7. Benefits: Permanent Residency and Naturalization {#section-7}

KIIP completion is one of the most effective ways to strengthen an F-5 or naturalization application.

### F-5 Permanent Residency Benefits

| Benefit | Requirement |
|---|---|

| Korean language requirement fulfilled | Certificate of KIIP completion accepted |

### Naturalization Benefits

| Benefit | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Written naturalization exam waived | Complete stage 5 |
| Interview advantage | KIIP completers receive preferential treatment |

### Other Benefits

| Visa Type | Benefit |
|---|---|

## 10. Frequently Asked Questions {#section-10}

**Q. Is KIIP mandatory for foreigners in Korea?**
A. No, participation is voluntary. However, if you plan to apply for F-5 permanent residency or naturalization, completing KIIP offers exam waivers and significantly strengthens your application.

**Q. Can I enroll if I know no Korean?**
A. Absolutely. Students with no Korean knowledge are assigned to Stage 0, which covers the Korean alphabet and basic expressions.

**Q. Can I take KIIP while working full-time?**
A. Yes. Many institutions offer evening and weekend classes. Some stages are also available online.

**Q. Can I skip stages if I have a TOPIK certificate?**
A. Yes. TOPIK holders can skip directly to the appropriate stage. For example, a TOPIK Level 5 holder only needs to complete Stage 5 (Korean Society Understanding).

**Q. What happens to my KIIP progress if my visa type changes?**
A. Completed stages remain valid. Your progress is tied to your alien registration number, not your visa type. Confirm any updated eligibility requirements with your immigration office after the visa change.
