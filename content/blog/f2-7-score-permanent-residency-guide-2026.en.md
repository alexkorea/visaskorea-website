## What Is the F-2-7 Points-Based Visa?

Korea's F-2-7 is a **points-based long-term residency visa** awarded to foreigners who accumulate 80 or more points out of 120. It serves as a pathway to the F-5 permanent residency visa after five years of continuous legal stay.

## 2026 Scoring Table Summary

| Category | Max Points |
|----------|-----------|
| Age | 20 |
| Education | 35 |
| Income / Tax | 20 |
| Korean Language (TOPIK) | 20 |
| Social Integration Program (KIIP) | 10 |
| Volunteer Activity | 5 |
| Bonus Points | +5 each |

## Key Scoring Areas

**Age**: Applicants aged 30–34 receive the maximum 20 points. Scores decrease for older applicants.

**Education**: A doctoral degree earns 35 points; a bachelor's earns 20. STEM or IT fields receive a bonus of +3 to +5 points.

**Income**: Annual income at or above 200% of Korea's per capita GNI (approximately KRW 84 million in 2026) earns the full 20 points.

**Korean Language**: TOPIK Level 6 = 20 points; Level 5 = 15 points. KIIP completion up to Stage 5 = 10 points. Only the higher of TOPIK or KIIP is counted.

## Eligibility Requirements

- Minimum 3 years of legal continuous stay in Korea
- No criminal convictions during the stay period
- Valid passport and current visa status

## How to Reach 80 Points

Focus first on TOPIK Level 5 or 6 (15–20 points) and ensure your income documentation is complete. Combine with a bachelor's or higher degree and age-based points to cross the threshold.
