# Korea D-10 Job Seeking Visa: How to Find Employment in Korea 2026

## What is the D-10 Visa?

The D-10 visa allows foreign nationals to legally stay in Korea while searching for employment. It is available to recent graduates from Korean universities and qualified foreign professionals with relevant work experience.

## Who Can Apply?

There are two main eligibility paths. First, foreign nationals who have graduated (or are about to graduate) from a Korean university with an associate's degree or higher. Second, foreign nationals who hold a bachelor's degree or higher from an overseas university and have relevant professional experience.

## Duration of Stay

The initial permit is 6 months, extendable up to 2 times (6 months each), for a maximum total stay of 18 months.

## Permitted Activities

D-10 visa holders may attend job fairs, participate in interviews, and enroll in vocational training programs. Actual paid employment requires changing to the appropriate work visa category.

## Transitioning to a Work Visa

Once employment is confirmed, you must apply to change your status to the appropriate work visa category (E-7, E-3, E-4, etc.) at the regional immigration office.
