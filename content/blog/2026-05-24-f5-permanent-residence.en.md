## F-5 Permanent Residence Visa Complete Guide

The F-5 (permanent residence) visa allows indefinite stay in Korea without periodic renewals.

### Key Eligibility Categories

**General Long-Term Residents** (5+ years legal stay):
- 5+ years of continuous legal residence
- Annual income above GNI level
- Korean language proficiency (TOPIK Level 3+)
- No criminal record

**F-2-7 → F-5 Conversion**: 3+ years on F-2-7 status while maintaining point requirements.

**F-4 → F-5 Conversion**: 2+ years on F-4 with income and health insurance requirements met.

**Investment Immigration**: KRW 500M+ in public benefit investment projects.

### Key Documents

Income proof and Korean language certification are the most challenging parts.

- Entire stay legality proof (immigration record)
- Income verification (12 months of health insurance payment)
- TOPIK score certificate (Level 3 minimum)
- Criminal record from home country and Korea
