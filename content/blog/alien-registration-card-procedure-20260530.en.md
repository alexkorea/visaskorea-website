## What is the Alien Registration Card (ARC)?

The Alien Registration Card (ARC) is an official identification document issued to foreigners who stay in Korea for more than 90 days, under Article 31 of the Immigration Control Act. Once registered, foreign nationals can use the ARC for administrative services, banking, mobile phone contracts, and many other essential services in Korea.

## Who Must Register?

Under Article 31 of the Immigration Control Act, the following foreign nationals must complete alien registration **within 90 days of entry**:

- Foreigners who entered Korea with a visa valid for more than 90 days
- Foreigners who changed their short-term visa to a long-term residence status
- Recognized refugees

Exceptions: Holders of Diplomatic (A-1), Official (A-2), and Agreement (A-3) status are exempt from registration.

## Application Procedure

### Step 1: Book an Appointment Online
- Reserve a visit at HiKorea (www.hikorea.go.kr) or Government24 (www.gov.kr)
- Attend in person at your local Immigration Office or Foreign Population Office

### Step 2: Prepare Required Documents
- **Original passport** (for residence status verification)
- **Application form** (available at the immigration office or downloadable from HiKorea)
- **1 passport-size photo** (3.5cm × 4.5cm, taken within 6 months, white background)
- **Fee**: KRW 30,000 (cash or card accepted)
- Additional documents may be required based on visa type (employment contract, enrollment certificate, etc.)

### Step 3: Visit the Immigration Office and Submit Application
- Arrive at your scheduled appointment time
- Complete the application form and submit to the officer
- Biometric registration (fingerprints and photo) will be taken on-site

### Step 4: Receive Your ARC
- The card is typically ready within **2–3 weeks** after application
- You will be notified via SMS or HiKorea notification
- In-person collection is required (proxy collection needs a power of attorney and ID of the proxy)

## ARC Renewal and Reissuance

### Renewal (When Extending Stay)
- ARC is renewed simultaneously when a stay extension is approved
- Submit the extension application at your local immigration office

### Reissuance (Lost or Damaged)
- Report the loss and apply for reissuance
- Reissuance fee: KRW 30,000
- Processing time: 2–3 weeks

### Address Change Notification
Under Article 36 of the Immigration Control Act, foreigners must report a change of address **within 14 days**.

## Penalties for Failure to Register

Under Article 94 of the Immigration Control Act, failure to comply with alien registration obligations may result in:
- A fine of up to **KRW 1 million**
- Disadvantage in stay extension reviews
- Risk of forced deportation for repeated violations
