# Korea Visa Overstay Penalties, Deportation & Re-entry Ban Guide 2026

Exceeding your authorized stay in Korea by even one day violates the Immigration Control Act. Voluntary departure before detection significantly reduces penalties.

---

## 1. Legal Definition

Your authorized stay ends on the date shown on your Alien Registration Card or entry stamp. The day after that date is already an overstay — with no grace period.

---

## 2. Fine Schedule

| Overstay Duration | Standard Fine | Voluntary Departure |
|-------------------|---------------|---------------------|
| 1–10 days | ₩100,000 | Waived or minimal |
| 11 days–1 month | ₩200,000 | ~50% reduction |
| 1–3 months | ₩300,000 | Partial reduction |
| 3–6 months | ₩500,000 | Limited reduction |
| Over 6 months | Up to ₩2,000,000 | Minimal reduction |

---

## 3. Re-entry Ban Periods

| Overstay Duration | Re-entry Ban |
|-------------------|-------------|
| Under 1 month | None or short |
| 1–3 months | 1 year |
| 3–6 months | 2 years |
| 6 months–1 year | 3 years |
| Over 1 year | 5+ years |

Forced deportation results in longer bans than voluntary departure.

---

## 4. Voluntary Departure Steps

1. Contact your local Immigration Office or use HiKorea (hikorea.go.kr)
2. Declare intent to depart voluntarily
3. Receive fine notice and pay
4. Receive an assigned departure date and depart
5. Confirm departure record

---

## 5. Voluntary vs. Forced Departure

| | Voluntary | Forced |
|--|-----------|--------|
| Fine | Reduced | Full |
| Re-entry ban | Shorter | Longer |
| Future visa | Recoverable | More difficult |
| Personal preparation | Possible | Immediate |

---

## 6. Re-applying After Overstay

Once the ban expires, reapplication is possible. However:

- **F-2-7 points visa**: Up to −20 pts deducted
- **E-series work visas**: Additional explanation documents may be required
- **F-6 marriage visa**: Stricter scrutiny of relationship genuineness

---

## 7. Prevention Checklist

- Begin extension procedures 90 days before expiry
- Report employer or address changes immediately
- Check remaining stay on HiKorea or Gov24 regularly
- If extension may be delayed, contact immigration in advance

---

## 8. FAQ

**Q. If I overstay while an extension application is pending?**
A. An extension application in processing typically protects your stay. Keep the receipt as proof.

**Q. Will unavoidable circumstances like hospitalization be considered?**
A. Contact immigration to explain the situation — humanitarian consideration may apply. Always consult in advance.

**Q. Can I work while overstaying?**
A. No. Working while overstaying adds an additional unauthorized activity violation.
