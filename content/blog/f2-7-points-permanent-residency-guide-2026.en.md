# F-2-7 Points-Based Permanent Residency Guide 2026

## What Is the F-2-7 Visa?

The F-2-7 is South Korea's **points-based residency visa**, designed to attract skilled foreign professionals who contribute to the Korean economy. Unlike employment-tied visas, the F-2-7 grants broad work freedom — holders can work in virtually any field without restriction.

Points are calculated across five main categories: age, education, income, occupation, and Korean language proficiency. Applicants who score **80 points or higher** out of a possible 120 qualify to apply. After holding F-2-7 status for **two years**, the pathway to F-5 permanent residency opens.

## 2026 Scoring Table

### 1. Age (Max 25 Points)

| Age Range | Points |
|-----------|--------|
| 18–24 | 20 |
| 25–29 | 25 |
| 30–34 | 20 |
| 35–39 | 15 |
| 40–44 | 10 |
| 45–49 | 5 |
| 50+ | 0 |

The 25–29 age bracket earns the maximum age score, making younger applicants naturally advantaged.

### 2. Education (Max 35 Points)

| Degree | General Field | STEM Field |
|--------|--------------|------------|
| PhD | 35 | 35 |
| Master's | 30 | 33 |
| Bachelor's | 20 | 23 |
| Associate's | 15 | 18 |

STEM disciplines (science, engineering, medicine, pharmacy) receive a **3-point bonus**. Foreign degrees must be officially recognized through NRCS (National Research Council of Science & Technology) before the bonus applies.

### 3. Income (Max 20 Points)

| Annual Income vs. Per Capita GNI | Points |
|----------------------------------|--------|
| 3× GNI or more | 20 |
| 2× GNI or more | 15 |
| 1× GNI or more | 10 |
| Below 1× GNI | 0 |

The 2026 per capita GNI benchmark is approximately **KRW 42 million** (Ministry of Justice figure). Rental and dividend income may be counted alongside employment income.

### 4. Occupation (Max 20 Points)

| Occupation Type | Points |
|-----------------|--------|
| OECD High-Skilled (Management / Professional) | 20 |
| Semi-Professional | 15 |
| General White-Collar | 10 |
| Other | 5 |

### 5. Korean Language Proficiency (Max 20 Points)

| TOPIK Level | Points |
|-------------|--------|
| Level 6 | 20 |
| Level 5 | 18 |
| Level 4 | 14 |
| Level 3 | 10 |
| Level 2 or below / None | 0 |

TOPIK scores are valid for **2 years** from the test date.

### 6. Bonus and Deduction Items

**Bonus points:**
- Graduated from a Korean 4-year university: +2
- Actively operating a Korean-registered business: +5
- National commendation or award: +5

**Deduction points:**
- Immigration violation history: −5 to −15
- Unpaid taxes: −5

## Minimum Score and Realistic Targets

The official qualifying threshold is **80 points**. However, during high-competition periods, applicants with **85–90+ points** are statistically more likely to receive smooth approvals. If you are close to the 80-point mark, improving your TOPIK score or documenting additional income can make a meaningful difference.

## Common Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Missing the STEM Education Bonus
Many applicants with foreign STEM degrees fail to complete the Korean degree recognition process and lose the 3-point bonus. Complete NRCS recognition before submitting your application.

### Mistake 2: Misunderstanding the Income Calculation Period
Income is assessed over the **12 months immediately before the application date** — not the previous tax year. Collect income certificates and withholding tax receipts that cover this exact period.

### Mistake 3: Submitting Expired TOPIK Scores
TOPIK certificates expire two years after the test date. Verify your certificate is still valid before applying.

### Mistake 4: Not Checking Visa Category Eligibility
F-2-7 is open to holders of certain visa types (D-2, E-1 through E-7, etc.). Some categories are restricted. Confirm eligibility for your specific current visa status before investing time in document preparation.

### Mistake 5: Overlooking Deduction Items
A prior immigration violation or tax arrears can reduce your score significantly. Resolve any outstanding taxes before applying and review your immigration record through HiKorea.

## Application Process

### Step 1: Pre-Check Your Score
Use the Ministry of Justice's score simulation tool on **HiKorea (www.hikorea.go.kr)** or the KIIP portal. Confirm that your current visa status qualifies for F-2-7 conversion.

### Step 2: Prepare Documents
- Passport and Alien Registration Card (ARC) copy
- Degree certificate + official Korean degree recognition document (for foreign degrees)
- Income verification: income confirmation certificate, withholding tax receipt, employment certificate
- TOPIK score certificate (within 2-year validity)
- Bonus-point supporting documents (if applicable)
- No-delinquency tax certificate

### Step 3: Submit the Application
- **Where:** Immigration office with jurisdiction over your address
- **Online option:** HiKorea online submission
- **Fee:** KRW 60,000

### Step 4: Await Decision
- Standard processing: **2–4 weeks**
- Additional documents may be requested; respond within 30 days
- Upon approval, receive F-2-7 status change permit or sticker

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1. Can F-2-7 holders work in any industry?**
Yes. The F-2-7 (Residency) visa places no restriction on the type of work you can do. This is one of its key advantages over employment-specific visas like E-7.

**Q2. Can my spouse and children join me?**
Yes. Once you hold F-2-7, your spouse and unmarried minor children can obtain **F-3 (Dependent) visas**, which allow them to reside in Korea with you.

**Q3. What if my score is below 80?**
Focus on the easiest levers: improve your TOPIK level (up to 20 points) or document higher income. Alternatively, explore other visa pathways such as E-7-1 (Special Occupation) depending on your profession.

**Q4. Are degrees from foreign universities accepted?**
Yes, but they must be officially recognized through the **National Research Council of Science & Technology (NRCS)**. Processing typically takes 2–4 weeks and must be completed before you apply.

**Q5. When can I apply for F-5 permanent residency?**
After maintaining F-2-7 status continuously for **2 years** (without significant absences or violations), you may apply for F-5 (Permanent Residency). Clean compliance during this period is essential.
