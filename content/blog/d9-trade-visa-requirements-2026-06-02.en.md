## What is the D-9 Visa?

The D-9 (Trade/Commercial) visa is issued for foreign nationals conducting trade-related activities with Korean companies — including executing import/export contracts, market research, and technical support.

## Eligibility

- Employees of foreign companies performing trade-related duties in Korea
- Foreign investors in Korea (note: D-8 applies to direct investment with incorporation)
- Companies with annual trade volume of at least USD 300,000

## Required Documents

| # | Document |
|---|----------|
| 1 | Visa application form |
| 2 | Valid passport (1+ year validity) |
| 3 | Passport photo |
| 4 | Trade records (contracts, bank transfer records) |
| 5 | Certificate of employment |
| 6 | Business registration or corporate registration |

## Stay Duration

Initial visa: up to 1 year. Renewals: 1–2 additional years depending on review.

## D-9 vs D-8

D-9 is for trade/commercial activities; D-8 is for direct foreign investment (company establishment). If you represent a foreign company without establishing a Korean entity, D-9 is appropriate.
