## What Is the E-7 Visa?

The E-7 (Specific Activities) visa is issued to foreign nationals who work in designated professional occupations specified by the Minister of Justice of Korea. It covers skilled and technical positions, not simple labor.

## Key Permitted Job Categories

| Field | Examples |
|-------|---------|
| IT / Software | Software developers, data analysts, system engineers |
| Business / Finance | Management consultants, corporate management |
| Education | Foreign language instructors (non-E-2), researchers |
| Healthcare | Licensed physicians, dentists (separate license required) |
| Manufacturing | Specialized technicians, aviation maintenance |
| Culture / Arts | Broadcast specialists, foreign cultural experts |

## Eligibility Requirements

### Employer Requirements
- Valid business registration
- Enrolled in employment insurance and industrial accident insurance

### Applicant Requirements
- Bachelor's degree or higher in a related field, OR 5+ years of relevant work experience
- Valid passport and current immigration status (if applying in Korea)

## Required Documents

1. Certificate of visa issuance (filed by employer)
2. Employment contract (signed by both parties)
3. Academic and career credentials (apostilled or embassy-certified)
4. Business registration certificate and corporate registry
5. Job category confirmation letter (if required by Ministry of Justice)
