# Korea Foreign Driver's License Conversion Guide 2026: Process & Documents by Country

Foreign nationals on long-term stays in Korea can convert their home country driver's license to a Korean license for more convenient driving. Korea has mutual recognition agreements with many countries, allowing license holders from those countries to convert without sitting any tests.

## 1. What Is Foreign Driver's License Conversion?

Foreign driver's license conversion is the process of exchanging your home country license for a Korean driver's license. Holding a Korean license allows you to drive domestically during long-term stays without needing to renew an International Driving Permit.

Benefits of conversion:
- No need to renew an IDP during long-term stays
- Easier rental car use and vehicle insurance enrollment
- Can also serve as supplementary ID alongside your ARC

---

## 2. Countries Eligible for No-Test Conversion

Holders of licenses from countries with mutual recognition agreements with Korea can convert without written or road tests.

| Region | Eligible Countries |
|---|---|
| Asia | Japan, Taiwan, Thailand, Singapore |
| Europe | Germany, France, UK, Netherlands, Belgium, Switzerland, Austria, Sweden, Denmark, Norway, Finland, Ireland, Spain, Italy, Portugal, Poland, Czech Republic, Slovakia, Hungary, and more |
| Americas | USA, Canada, Chile, Mexico |
| Oceania | Australia, New Zealand |

> Agreement terms may change. Always verify the current list of eligible countries at the Korea Road Traffic Authority (KoROAD) or the Driver's License Test Management Office before applying.

---

## 3. Countries Requiring Tests

Applicants from countries without a mutual recognition agreement with Korea may be required to pass a written test, skills test, or both.

| Country Type | Conversion Method |
|---|---|
| Agreement countries | No-test conversion (document review only) |
| Non-agreement (written exam waived) | Skills test only |
| Non-agreement (full testing required) | Both written and skills tests required |

## 5. Required Documents

| Document | Notes |
|---|---|
| Original foreign driver's license | Valid license |
| Certified Korean translation | Notarized translation or authentication from home country consulate (some countries exempt) |
| Original passport | Currently valid |
| Alien Registration Card | If applicable |
| Passport photo | Taken within 6 months, white background |
| Fee | Varies by license class |
| Physical examination result | Required for some license classes (large/special vehicles) |

> For some countries (e.g., Japan, China), a certificate of license authentication issued by the home country consulate or embassy in Korea may be required. Confirm in advance.

## 6. Where and How to Apply

### Application Locations

| Location | Notes |
|---|---|
| Driver's license test centers (nationwide) | All conversion applications accepted |
| KoROAD regional offices | Available at some locations |

### Application Steps

1. **Prepare documents** — Gather all documents including translations and notarizations
2. **Visit driver's license test center** — Go to the foreign license conversion counter
3. **Document review** — Documents and license validity verified
4. **Physical examination** — Basic vision and hearing check on-site
5. **Pay fee**
6. **License issued** — Issued immediately or within a few days

| Item | Details |
|---|---|

| Renewal | Visit driver's license center before expiry |
| Home country license after conversion | You keep your home country license — no need to return it |

## 8. Consequences of Driving Without a Valid License

| Situation | Details |
|---|---|
| Driving without a license | Criminal penalty (up to 1 year imprisonment or fine up to KRW 3 million) |
| Driving after IDP expiry | Same penalty as driving without any license |
| Using foreign license after 1 year of residence | Not permitted for domestic driving in Korea |

## 9. International Driving Permit (IDP) Usage

An IDP is a temporary driving authorization issued in your home country.

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Validity | 1 year from date of issue |
| Usage in Korea | Valid for driving in Korea for up to 1 year from entry |
| Where to get one | Issue by your home country's road traffic authority (must be obtained before entering Korea) |
| Long-term stays | Must convert to Korean license after 1 year |

## 10. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. Can I drive in Korea immediately on my foreign license?**
A. Yes. For up to 1 year after entry, you may drive using an IDP (International Driving Permit) or your home country license with a certified Korean translation. After 1 year, you must convert to a Korean license.

**Q. Do I really not need to take any tests for agreement-country licenses?**
A. Correct. Holders of licenses from countries with mutual recognition agreements with Korea can convert through document review only — no written or skills test required. Documents must be properly prepared and the license must be valid.

**Q. Do I have to return my home country license after conversion?**
A. No. You keep your home country license after converting to a Korean license. You can continue to use it when driving in your home country.

**Q. How do I renew my Korean license after conversion?**
A. Korean driver's licenses must be renewed every 10 years (every 5 years for those 65 and older). The renewal process involves a physical examination.

**Q. Is a Chinese driver's license convertible without a test?**
A. As of 2024, China does not have a mutual recognition agreement with Korea, so a written or skills test is required. Always confirm the current list of eligible countries with KoROAD.
