# E-7 Visa Extension and Salary Requirements Guide Korea 2026

The E-7 (Specific Activities) visa is for foreign professionals employed in designated occupations. Renewal depends heavily on maintaining employment and meeting salary benchmarks.

---

## 1. Basic Requirements for E-7 Renewal

- **Continued employment:** Same employer or same occupation (job changes require notification)
- **Salary threshold:** Must meet the minimum salary for your occupation category
- **Application timing:** Apply up to 4 months before expiry
- **Tax compliance:** Previous year's income tax filed and paid

---

## 2. Salary Standards

E-7 salary requirements are tied to GNI (Gross National Income per capita).

| Occupation | Minimum Monthly Salary |
|-----------|----------------------|
| IT / Engineering | GNI 80%+ (~₩2.8M) |
| Accounting / Legal / Consulting | GNI 80%+ |
| Arts / Design | GNI 70%+ |
| Korean cuisine chef | GNI 70%+ |
| Other professionals | GNI 80%+ |

The 2025 monthly GNI benchmark is approximately ₩3.5 million. GNI 80% = approximately ₩2.8M/month.

## 5. Changing Employers

E-7 is tied to a specific employer and occupation.

- **Workplace change notification:** Required within 15 days of changing jobs
- **Occupation change:** Requires prior approval even within E-7 permitted categories
- **Failure to report:** Risk of visa violation, fines, or deportation

---

## 6. E-7 to E-7-4 (Skilled Worker) Conversion

Qualifying E-7 holders can convert to E-7-4, which provides greater long-term stability.

- **Requirements:** Points-based system (employment duration, salary, Korean language, certifications)
- **Benefit:** Faster pathway to F-2 long-term residency

---

## 7. FAQ

**Q. Can I renew if my salary is below the threshold?**
A. Generally not. Consult with the immigration office in advance and negotiate a raise with your employer if possible.

**Q. Can I travel abroad while my renewal is pending?**
A. Yes, if you have re-entry permission or a multiple-entry visa.

**Q. Can freelancers apply under E-7?**
A. E-7 requires an employment contract, so freelance arrangements are generally not eligible.
