# D-8 Corporate Investment Visa Korea: Requirements and Application Guide 2026

## What is the D-8 Visa?

The D-8 (Corporate Investment) visa is issued to foreign investors who have established or invested in a Korean company and need to reside in Korea to manage or operate their business.

## Eligibility Requirements

To qualify for a D-8 visa:

- **Minimum Investment**: KRW 100 million or more (company establishment or capital increase)
- **Company Type**: Korean corporation (Ltd., LLC, etc.)
- **FDI Registration**: Completed foreign investment notification with a foreign exchange bank or KOTRA
- **Active Management**: Registered as a director or representative of the corporation

## Investment Amount Criteria

The paid-in capital must be at least KRW 100 million, with proof of actual payment. Investments of USD 500,000 or more qualify without a requirement to create new employment.

## Required Documents

- Visa application form
- Passport
- Corporate registration certificate (등기부등본)
- Foreign-invested enterprise registration certificate
- Business registration certificate
- Capital payment proof (bank statement, etc.)
- Business plan

## Application Process

1. Establish a Korean corporation and complete FDI notification
2. Register as a director or representative
3. Apply for D-8 visa at the local Immigration Office
4. Receive residence permit upon approval
