# F-5 Permanent Residency Korea 2026 — Complete Guide

Korea's F-5 visa grants unlimited residency without periodic renewals. The right pathway depends on your current visa status and length of stay.

## Main F-5 Pathways

| Route | Key Requirements |
|-------|-----------------|
| F-5-1 (Long Stay) | 5+ years lawful residence |
| F-5-7 (Overseas Korean) | F-4 + 2 years + KRW 300M assets |
| F-5-16 (Points System) | F-2-7 + 3 years + 2× GNI income |
| F-5-18 (High Investment) | KRW 500M+ direct investment |

## Disqualifying Factors
- Prison sentence within past 5 years
- Outstanding tax debt
- Deportation within past 3 years

## Application Steps
1. Verify eligibility
2. Prepare documents (4–6 weeks)
3. Visit local immigration office
4. Wait for review (4–12 weeks)
5. Receive F-5 residence card
