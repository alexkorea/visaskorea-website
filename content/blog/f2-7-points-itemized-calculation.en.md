# Complete Breakdown of F-2 Residence Visa Point System Calculation by Category

The F-2-7 residence visa requires at least 80 out of a maximum 120 points to qualify, but in practice there is a clear dividing line even among applicants who clear the 80-point threshold.

This is for foreigners legally residing in Korea who can accumulate points in the education, Korean language, income, and age categories. The first thing to know is that this is not a simple sum — each category has narrow recognition criteria.

This article covers the scoring criteria by category, the deduction points that are most often overlooked in practice, and practical ways to make up for shortfalls when your score falls short.

<h2 id="section-1">Basic Structure of the F-2-7 Point System</h2>

The F-2-7 point system is divided into base categories and bonus/deduction categories.

The base categories consist of four items — education, Korean language proficiency, annual income, and age — and 80 points combined is the first-pass threshold.

Bonus categories include volunteer work, completion of the Social Integration Program, and domestic study experience, while deductions arise from immigration law violations, tax delinquency, and criminal records.

### The Key to Total Score Calculation

Here is the key point.

Just clearing 80 points isn't the end — there are categories where you cannot score 0 in even a single slot.

In actual reviews, if your Korean language score is 0, no matter how high your other category scores are, you will be disqualified.

### Distinguishing Mandatory and Optional Categories

Education, Korean language, income, and age are all mandatory scoring categories.

If you score 0 in any one of these, application itself is often difficult, and detailed criteria for this part change every year as posted on the [HiKorea announcements page](https://www.hikorea.go.kr).

The criteria applied this year may be guided differently by each immigration office, so you'll need a consultation to confirm the criteria that fit your case.

<h2 id="section-2">How Education Points Are Calculated</h2>

Education is recognized up to a maximum of 35 points.

| Education Level | Points Recognized | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Doctoral degree | 35 pts | Both domestic and overseas recognized |
| Master's degree | 32 pts | Separate bonus for domestic degree |
| Bachelor's degree | 25 pts | 4-year regular program |
| Associate degree | 20 pts | 2–3 year program |
| High school graduate | 15 pts | Base recognition |

### Bonus Points for Domestic Degrees

Degrees obtained in Korea receive additional bonus points.

A domestic bachelor's degree adds +5 points, and master's and doctoral degrees are added differently by category.

In fact, applicants with only overseas degrees may appear to have sufficient education points, but they often fall behind in total score when compared to those with domestic degrees.

### Where Degree Recognition Procedures Get Stuck

For overseas degrees, you need graduation certificates and transcripts that have gone through apostille or consular authentication for educational recognition.

The most common case is having all the documents ready but the degree name translation doesn't match the Korean academic system, so the recognized level drops one grade.

If this part is weak, a doctoral degree can actually be assessed as a master's degree, so review at the degree recognition stage is essential beforehand.

<h2 id="section-3">How Korean Language Proficiency Points Are Calculated</h2>

Korean language scores are verified through TOPIK or completion of the Social Integration Program (KIIP).

| Verification Method | Points | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| TOPIK Level 5–6 / KIIP Stage 5 completion | 20 pts | Highest score |
| TOPIK Level 4 / KIIP Stage 4 completion | 15 pts | |
| TOPIK Level 3 / KIIP Stage 3 completion | 10 pts | |
| TOPIK Level 2 / KIIP Stage 2 completion | 5 pts | Minimum recognition |
| TOPIK Level 1 or unqualified | 0 pts | Not eligible to apply |

### Difference Between TOPIK and KIIP

TOPIK gives you points immediately based on test results, while KIIP accumulates points through stage-by-stage class completion.

In practice, KIIP completion is less burdensome for applicants busy with study or work, but the attendance requirements per stage are strict.

KIIP stage recognition reflects records on the [Social Integration Information Network](https://www.socinet.go.kr) directly, so if absences accumulate in the middle, your stage drops one level.

### Where Korean Language Points Often Get Stuck

The first thing to check is whether your TOPIK score is still within its validity period.

TOPIK scores are valid for 2 years from the issue date, and expired scores are not recognized.

In a recent similar case, an applicant held TOPIK Level 5 but the validity had expired, resulting in 0 points and the application being blocked entirely.

<h2 id="section-4">How Income Points Are Calculated</h2>

Income is calculated based on the previous year's annual income.

Points are divided by the ratio of your annual income to GNI (Gross National Income per capita), with per-capita GNI based on figures published by the [Bank of Korea Economic Statistics System](https://ecos.bok.or.kr).

### Income Verification Documents

Income verification goes in two directions.

Wage earners: Withholding tax receipt for wage income issued by [Hometax](https://www.hometax.go.kr)

Business income earners: Comprehensive income tax filing confirmation + tax payment certificate

In practice, the most common sticking point is when income exists but filing has been omitted, resulting in a 0 score.

### The Income Point Trap

The threshold amounts for income points change each year based on GNI fluctuations, so please confirm this year's exact threshold through consultation.

Freelancers and sole proprietors in particular often find that even with high revenue, their comprehensive income amount after expense deductions is low, leaving them short on points.

If you keep F-2-7 in mind from the time of tax filing, reviewing the scope of expense deductions directly affects your score.

> **Practical Tip:** If your F-2-7 application plan is set just before comprehensive income filing, the filing method directly impacts your score.

<h2 id="section-5">Age and Other Bonus Categories</h2>

Age is recognized up to a maximum of 25 points, with lower scores for ages 18–24 and 50 and above.

| Age Group | Points |
|---|---|
| 25–29 | 20 pts |
| 30–34 | 25 pts |
| 35–39 | 23 pts |
| 40–44 | 20 pts |
| 45–49 | 18 pts |
| 50 and above | 15 pts |

### Summary of Bonus Categories

Bonuses arise from the following items.

Volunteer activity record (50+ hours per year)

Completion of KIIP Stage 5

Domestic 4-year bachelor's degree or higher

Employment in occupations classified as overseas excellent talent

Bonus categories are cumulative, so when your score is borderline, securing bonus points is faster than trying to push up education or income.

### Why Volunteer Records Are Frequently Missed

Volunteer records are only recognized for hours registered with [1365 Volunteer Portal](https://www.1365.go.kr) or [VMS Social Welfare Volunteer](https://www.vms.or.kr).

Unofficial volunteer activities don't count toward points no matter how many hours you put in.

The registering organizations and recognition scope may change, so confirmation before application is needed.

<h2 id="section-6">Deduction Categories and Frequently Overlooked Points</h2>

Deductions arise from immigration law violations, tax delinquency, and criminal records.

| Reason for Deduction | Deduction Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Immigration law violation (fine) | -5 to -30 pts | Cumulative by violation count |
| Tax delinquency | -10 pts | Recovers after a certain period post-payment |
| Criminal punishment (fine or higher) | -10 to -50 pts | Based on sentence confirmation date |
| Simple administrative fine | Deduction may apply | Judged case by case |

### Where Immigration Law Violation Deductions Get Stuck

Even past warning dispositions you weren't aware of remain on record as immigration law violations and result in deductions.

Light violations like administrative fines for delayed extension of stay or failure to report a change of residence also fall under deduction.

In actual reviews, this is usually the most common case where applicants fail to clear the 80-point pass line as these surface.

> **Note:** You can check your own violation history through an immigration information disclosure request, but deduction conversion varies according to [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr) operating guidelines.

### Recovery Timeline for Tax Delinquency Deductions

Even after tax payment, deductions remain for a certain period.

The deduction doesn't disappear immediately upon payment — points recover only after a certain period has passed post-payment.

The recovery period varies case by case, so confirmation of your own case is necessary.

<h2 id="section-7">Recovery Strategies When Your Score Falls Short</h2>

When your score is insufficient, there are two fastest recovery routes.

### Raising Your Korean Language Score

TOPIK has fixed test schedules and results take time to arrive, but KIIP Stage 5 completion can be finished quickly based on your own pace.

In particular, KIIP is a category where Korean language points + bonus points are captured simultaneously, separate from the education bonus, making it the most efficient option for score recovery.

### Strengthening Bonus Categories

Volunteer hours are a category that can be filled in a short period.

For the overseas excellent talent occupation bonus, you need to check whether your occupation falls under the [KQ-recognized occupations of HRD Korea](https://www.hrdkorea.or.kr).

The occupation classification is renewed annually, so check through consultation whether your case falls under a bonus-eligible occupation.

<h2 id="section-8">Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)</h2>

**Q1. If my F-2-7 score is exactly 80, am I guaranteed to pass?**

A. 80 points is the application qualification threshold, not a guarantee of passing.

During review, scores may drop due to readjustment of category recognition scopes, and additional documents may be requested depending on the case.

**Q2. If I have both TOPIK and KIIP, are the scores combined?**

A. They are not combined.

Only the higher of the two is recognized, and you must submit the one more favorable to you.

**Q3. Is a doctoral degree worth 35 points even if it's not a Korean degree?**

A. The doctoral degree itself is 35 points regardless.

However, the domestic degree bonus is a separate category, so applicants with only overseas degrees miss out on that bonus.

**Q4. How many volunteer hours do I need to accumulate for points?**

A. A certain number of annual hours must be accumulated for recognition, and registration with an authorized organization is a precondition.

Unofficial volunteer work doesn't count toward points no matter how many hours you put in.

**Q5. Does my personal bank account balance count as income?**

A. It does not count.

Points are calculated only based on wage income or comprehensive income amount that has been reported to the tax authorities.

**Q6. Can deductions be offset by bonus points?**

A. There are cases where it is possible.

However, major deductions like criminal punishment are not offset by simple bonuses, and case-by-case review is needed.
