# How to Improve Your F-2-7 Points Score in Korea 2026

The F-2-7 points-based residency visa uses a 120-point system. Most applicants need at least 80 points to qualify. The key is identifying which categories you can realistically improve before applying.

---

## 1. Scoring Overview

| Category | Max Points |
|----------|-----------|
| Age | 20 |
| Education | 30 |
| Income | 20 |
| Korean proficiency | 20 |
| Certifications | 10 |
| Volunteer & tax bonus | up to 10 |
| **Total** | **120 (threshold: 80)** |

---

## 2. Age — Fixed, But Sets Your Baseline

- **25–29**: 20 pts
- **30–34**: 18 pts
- **35–39**: 16 pts
- **40–44**: 12 pts
- **45–49**: 8 pts
- **50+**: 4 pts

Applicants over 40 must maximize income, Korean, and bonus points to compensate.

---

## 3. Income — The Fastest Category to Move

Evaluated using the prior year's income from the National Tax Service certificate. The 2025 GNI benchmark is approximately ₩42 million.

| Annual Income | Points |
|--------------|--------|
| GNI 300%+ (₩126M+) | 20 |
| GNI 200–300% (₩84M–₩126M) | 16 |
| GNI 150–200% (₩63M–₩84M) | 12 |
| GNI 100–150% (₩42M–₩63M) | 8 |
| GNI 80–100% | 4 |

---

## 4. Korean Proficiency — TOPIK or KIIP

| Level | Points |
|-------|--------|
| TOPIK 6 | 20 |
| TOPIK 5 / KIIP Stage 5 | 16 |
| TOPIK 4 / KIIP Stage 4 | 12 |
| TOPIK 3 | 8 |
| TOPIK 2 | 4 |

TOPIK 4 is the most achievable target for most applicants. KIIP is free but takes longer.

---

## 5. Education — STEM Bonus

| Degree | General | STEM |
|--------|---------|------|
| PhD | 30 | 30 |
| Master's | 25 | 28 |
| Bachelor's | 18 | 22 |
| Associate's | 10 | 13 |
| High school or below | 5 | 5 |

---

## 6. Certifications

Korean national technical qualifications (Engineer-level or above) or professional licenses earn up to 10 points.

---

## 7. Volunteer & Tax Bonus

- 200+ volunteer hours/year: 10 pts
- 100+ hours/year: 7 pts
- 50+ hours/year: 5 pts
- 3+ years of diligent tax filing: 3 pts

Volunteer hours must be logged through registered organizations on the 1365 portal.

---

## 8. Deduction Items

- Overstay history: up to −20 pts
- Criminal record: up to −10 pts or more
- Unauthorized activity: −5 pts

---

## 9. Sample Calculation — Age 42

42 / GNI 180% / Bachelor's (general) / TOPIK 4 / 60 volunteer hrs → 12+12+18+12+5 = **59 pts** — below threshold.

Path to 80: TOPIK 5 (+4) → 63, GNI 200% (+4) → 67, 100 volunteer hrs (+2) → 69, national certification (+10) → 79, 3yr tax diligence (+3) → **82 pts**.

---

## 10. FAQ

**Q. Can I self-check my score before applying?**
A. Yes — HiKorea (hikorea.go.kr) offers a self-assessment tool.

**Q. Is there a waiting period after a rejected F-2-7 application?**
A. No mandatory waiting period, but you should improve your score before reapplying.

**Q. Do overseas degrees count?**
A. Yes, with a credential evaluation from a recognized agency.
