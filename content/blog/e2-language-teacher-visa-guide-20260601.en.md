# E-2 Language Teacher Visa Korea: Complete Guide 2026

## What is the E-2 Visa?

The E-2 (Conversation Instructor) visa is issued to foreign nationals who teach foreign languages in Korea. It is commonly used by English, Chinese, and Japanese instructors at language academies (hagwons), schools, and kindergartens.

## Eligibility Requirements

To qualify for an E-2 visa, applicants must meet the following criteria:

- **Nationality**: Citizens of English-speaking countries (USA, UK, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Ireland, South Africa) for English instruction
- **Education**: Bachelor's degree or higher
- **Background**: Clean criminal record in home country and Korea
- **Health**: Negative results for drug and HIV tests

## Required Documents

- Visa application form
- Passport (valid for at least 6 months)
- One passport photo
- Diploma and transcripts (apostilled or consular-certified)
- Criminal record check (apostilled or consular-certified)
- Employment contract from school/academy
- Medical examination report from a designated hospital

## Application Process

1. Secure a job offer and sign an employment contract with a Korean school or academy
2. Employer issues a letter of invitation
3. Apply at the Korean embassy in your home country
4. Enter Korea after visa issuance

## Visa Duration and Renewal

Initial E-2 visas are issued for one year and can be renewed annually as long as you remain with the same employer. Changing employers requires a separate activity permit or status change application.
