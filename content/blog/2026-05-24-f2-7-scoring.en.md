## F-2-7 Points-Based Resident Visa Strategy

The F-2-7 (Points-Based Talented Person) visa allows free employment and business activities in Korea without changing your status.

### Scoring Structure (120 points total)

| Category | Maximum |
|----------|---------|
| Age | 25 pts |
| Education | 35 pts |
| Korean Language | 20 pts |
| Income | 20 pts |
| Bonus Points | 20 pts |

**Age Scoring**: Under 29 earns maximum 25 points, decreasing with age.

**Education**: PhD earns 35 points; STEM Bachelor's earns 27 points (3-point STEM bonus).

**Korean Language (TOPIK)**: Level 6 = 20 pts, Level 5 = 18 pts, Level 4 = 14 pts.

**Income**: Based on previous year income vs GNI. Proven via health insurance records or tax certificates.

### Practical Tips

Most applicants struggle with income documentation. Employees use withholding tax receipts; self-employed use comprehensive income tax returns.

Plan TOPIK exam dates at least 6 months before your intended application date.
