# Korea F-2-7 Points-Based Residence Visa 2026

The F-2-7 visa is awarded to foreigners who score **80+ points** out of 170 across age, education, Korean ability, and income categories. It serves as the main stepping stone from work visas to permanent residency.

## 2026 Score Table

| Category | Max Points |
|----------|-----------|
| Age (24 or under = 25pts, 50+ = 0pts) | 25 |
| Education (PhD = 35pts, high school = 0pts) | 35 |
| Korean (TOPIK 6 = 20pts, level 2 or below = 0pts) | 20 |
| Annual Income (4× GNI = 30pts) | 30 |
| Bonus (KIIP +10, STEM degree +5, etc.) | 30 |

## Eligibility
Must fall into one of: listed company employee, growth-industry worker, licensed professional, domestic degree graduate currently employed, or recognized talent.

## Path to Permanent Residency (F-5-16)
- Hold F-2-7 for 3+ years
- Annual income ≥ 2× Korea's GNI
- Pass KIIP immigration integration test
