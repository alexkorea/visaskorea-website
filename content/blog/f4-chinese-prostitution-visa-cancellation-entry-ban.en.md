# Chinese-Korean F-4 Prostitution Violation Review: Complete Guide to Visa Cancellation and Entry Ban Procedures

When a Chinese-Korean F-4 visa holder is caught for prostitution-related offenses, an immigration violation review operates separately from criminal punishment, leading to visa cancellation, deportation, and entry ban.

Simple buyers, sellers, and brokers/venue providers are all subject to violation review, and the direction of immigration disposition starts diverging from the moment of police questioning.

This guide walks through the procedures right after being caught, the criteria for visa cancellation, how the entry ban period is determined, and the possibility of lifting re-entry restrictions — based on actual practice.

<h2 id="section-1">How F-4 Prostitution Violation Review Unfolds</h2>

### When the Violation Review Begins

Once caught during a prostitution crackdown, police investigation comes first.

The moment the police report is transmitted to the [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr), the violation review phase effectively begins.

A commonly overlooked point is that immigration can move first, even before criminal proceedings conclude.

Even if you are released with only a summary indictment and a fine, deportation and entry ban are reviewed separately.

### Separation of Criminal and Immigration Dispositions

Criminal proceedings deal with whether the [Act on the Punishment of Acts of Arranging Sexual Traffic](https://law.go.kr) has been violated, while violation review deals with whether your residence status can be maintained.

In practice, even when the criminal outcome is a light fine, visa cancellation often proceeds anyway.

The key point is that immigration does not simply follow the criminal outcome — the two procedures run on independent tracks.

<h2 id="section-2">Criteria for F-4 Visa Cancellation</h2>

### Application of Article 89 of the Immigration Control Act

A prostitution-related arrest is reviewed under Article 89 of the [Immigration Control Act](https://law.go.kr) as a ground for cancellation of residence status.

Under the violation review standards published by [HiKorea](https://www.hikorea.go.kr), violations involving public morals are treated as aggravating factors.

A single instance of buying sex can shake F-4 status, and brokering or venue provision almost always leads to cancellation.

### Why the Factual Record Is Decisive

What matters more than the paperwork is what facts get recorded in the police report.

Outcomes vary significantly depending on whether the case is logged as simple buying, or whether brokering, repetition, and transaction amounts are included.

If your explanation is inadequate, aggravating factors you never intended end up being forwarded to immigration as-is.

The criteria applied this year may be adjusted slightly each year, so confirm the exact application to your case through a consultation.

<h2 id="section-3">How Deportation and Entry Ban Periods Are Decided</h2>

### Typical Entry Ban Periods Applied

For prostitution cases, entry bans typically range from 5 to 10 years.

When brokering, repeat offenses, or minor-related elements overlap, the ban can extend beyond that range.

| Type of Offense | Typical Entry Ban Period | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Single instance of buying sex | Short to mid-term | Varies by case |
| Caught selling sex | Mid-term | Aggravated if repeated |
| Brokering / venue provision | Long-term | Permanent ban possible |
| Minor-related | Long-term to permanent | Aggravating factor |

The table above shows the typical practical range; the exact number of years is determined according to the disposition criteria of the Korea Immigration Service.

### Aggravating and Mitigating Factors

Aggravating factors include repetition, commercial intent, transaction amounts, and circumstances involving minors or coercion.

Mitigating factors include first-time offense, confession and remorse, family ties in Korea, and a long record of legal residence.

In actual review, even when both sets of factors apply, a single aggravating item is often enough to make the outcome heavy.

> **Caution:** Even if you casually state in the police stage that "it was just one time," credit card records and messenger logs often surface, reconstructing the case as a pattern of repetition.

<h2 id="section-4">What You Should Do Right After Being Caught</h2>

### Where Statements Often Go Wrong

This is where the problem starts.

If you guess at the number of times, amounts, and identities during police questioning, those guesses are passed on directly as material for the violation review.

For things you don't remember, it is safer to say you don't know than to estimate.

In particular, be especially careful with statements about brokers or introducers, as there is a risk of being reclassified as a broker yourself.

### Where Criminal Lawyers and Administrative Agents Differ

Criminal defense attorneys focus on reducing sentences and fines, while administrative agents focus on preventing the immigration disposition.

Because the two procedures run separately, it is common for the visa to be canceled even when the criminal side is well handled.

In practice, the key is to set the direction of your statement from the criminal questioning stage with the immigration disposition already in mind.

<h2 id="section-5">Re-entry and the Possibility of Lifting the Entry Ban</h2>

### Timing of a Petition to Lift the Entry Ban

Besides simply waiting out the entry ban, you can also consider filing a petition to lift it.

However, the acceptance rate is generally low for public morals cases, and filing too early almost always results in rejection.

The usual flow is to wait a certain period and then file with bundled materials on family ties, treatment, and remorse.

### Humanitarian Grounds and Proof of Family Ties

If you have a spouse or minor children in Korea, the scope for considering humanitarian grounds widens slightly.

Rather than writing lengthy arguments, organizing concrete documentation of family ties and actual cohabitation/support tends to produce better results.

Whether humanitarian grounds can apply to your case depends on your family composition, so a preliminary review should come first.

<h2 id="section-6">Where Practitioners Most Often Get Stuck</h2>

### The Line Between Simple Buying and Brokering

Even when caught at the same scene, the outcome differs greatly depending on whether the case ends as simple buying or is treated as brokering.

This is where the difference shows.

If even one line about introduction, connection, or venue provision enters the record, the entry ban period can more than double.

### Can You Switch to Another Residence Status After Losing F-4?

When F-4 is canceled, people often think of switching to H-2 or another status, but if a violation review record remains, new visa applications themselves get blocked.

While the entry ban is active, new issuance under any status is generally blocked.

This area varies by case, so the right next step is to plan only after reviewing your arrest record and disposition notice.

> **Practical Tip:** Securing a copy of the police report before the disposition notice arrives allows you to identify points to contest in advance during the violation review stage.

<h2 id="section-7">Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)</h2>

### Q1. Will my F-4 visa be canceled even if I only get a fine?

Even with just a summary indictment and a fine, immigration may conduct a separate violation review and decide on visa cancellation and deportation.

### Q2. If I voluntarily leave the country after being caught, am I exempt from the entry ban?

Voluntary departure does not exempt you from the entry ban.

However, the case may be processed as a departure order rather than deportation, which can create a difference on the record.

### Q3. Will the entry ban be lifted if I have family in Korea?

Simply having a spouse or minor children does not automatically lift the ban.

It can, however, work as a supporting factor when filing on humanitarian grounds.

### Q4. If I'm found not guilty in criminal court, will the visa cancellation also be reversed?

A criminal acquittal does not automatically reverse the immigration disposition.

You would have to challenge it separately through administrative appeal or administrative litigation.

### Q5. When is the right time to file a petition to lift the entry ban?

The right timing depends on the weight of the case and your personal circumstances, and filing too early almost always leads to rejection.

The appropriate timing for your case should be set after reviewing the disposition notice and records.
