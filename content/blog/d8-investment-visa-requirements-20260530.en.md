## What is the D-8 Business Investment Visa?

The D-8 visa (Corporate Investment) is issued to foreign investors who invest in Korean companies pursuant to the Foreign Investment Promotion Act, and to those who engage in management, operations, or technical fields at foreign-invested companies. It is governed by Annex 1 of the Enforcement Decree of the Immigration Control Act of Korea.

## Eligibility Requirements

### Investment Amount
- A minimum investment of **KRW 100 million (approximately USD 75,000)** is required under Article 2, Paragraph 1, Item 4 of the Foreign Investment Promotion Act.
- Investment may take the form of equity acquisition or long-term loan provision.
- The company must be registered as a foreign-invested enterprise.

### Employment Requirement
- The company must employ at least **2 Korean nationals**, or provide a verified employment plan.
- Exceptions apply for technology-intensive industries.

### Eligible Applicant Categories
1. **Executives/Managers**: CEO, directors, and senior management of the foreign-invested company
2. **Technical Specialists**: Professionals with a master's degree or higher, or 5+ years of experience in production/technology fields
3. **Trade-based investors**: Representatives of investment corporations engaged in trade activities

## Application Process

### Step 1: Register as Foreign-Invested Enterprise
- File a foreign investment declaration with KOTRA or a designated foreign exchange bank
- Establish a corporation and obtain the Foreign-Invested Enterprise Registration Certificate

### Step 2: Prepare Required Documents
- Visa issuance authorization application form (Form No. 21)
- Passport copy
- Foreign-Invested Enterprise Registration Certificate
- Investment verification documents (share acquisition certificate, etc.)
- Corporate registration certificate
- Employee roster or employment contracts (to verify Korean national employment)
- Certificate of employment or executive appointment confirmation

### Step 3: Apply for Visa Issuance Authorization
- Submit to the local Immigration Office or Foreign Population Office (Hi Korea)
- Processing time: 3–5 business days with complete documents

### Step 4: Apply for Visa (Overseas) or Change Status (Domestic)
- Present the Visa Issuance Authorization at a Korean embassy abroad to apply for the D-8 visa
- Alternatively, apply for a status change at the local immigration office if already in Korea

## Stay Duration and Renewal

- Initial stay period: 1 to 3 years depending on investment size and business status
- Extension: Apply at the local immigration office from 4 months before expiry
- Business performance and continued Korean employment are key criteria for renewal

## Important Notes

Under Article 25 of the Immigration Control Act, D-8 visa holders engaging in activities outside their authorized scope must obtain separate permission. Additionally, if investment is withdrawn or the business is closed, the visa may be cancelled. We strongly recommend consulting a licensed immigration agent before making any changes.
