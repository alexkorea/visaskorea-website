The F-2-7 points-based permanent residency system assigns different scores based on age. Even with identical income and education, your strategy should differ depending on your age group.

## Age Scoring Structure

Based on the F-2-7 evaluation table from the Korea Immigration Service:

| Age | Points |
|---|---|
| 25–34 | 15 points |
| 35–44 | 10 points |
| 45–54 | 5 points |
| 55 and above | 0 points |

## Strategy by Age Group

**Ages 25–34**: You receive the maximum 15 age points. Focus on building stable income, education, and Korean language scores to comfortably reach the 80-point threshold.

**Ages 35–44**: You lose 5 points from the maximum. Compensate by reaching higher income brackets (3× GNI or above) or obtaining TOPIK Level 4+ for language points.

**Ages 45–54**: You lose 10 points. Maximize education/qualification bonus points (PhD, professional licenses). Use tax payment history and social contribution bonus points actively.

**Ages 55+**: Zero age points. You must accumulate 80 points entirely from income, education, and contribution categories. Pre-assessment with a licensed agent is essential before applying.

## Optimizing Income Points

The income category awards up to 20 points and is calculated based on prior-year taxable income or national health insurance premium payments. When age points are low, maximizing income points becomes critical.
