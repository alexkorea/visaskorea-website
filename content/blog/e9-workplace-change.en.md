# E-9 Non-Professional Work Visa: Workplace Change Procedures and Requirements — 2026 Practical Guide

Changing workplaces on an E-9 non-professional work visa without prior approval from the Ministry of Employment and Labor constitutes illegal employment.
Unauthorized job changes are grounds for deportation and entry ban, and employers who hire such workers also face criminal penalties.
This guide covers permitted reasons for workplace changes, the maximum number of changes allowed, specific procedures, required documents, processing times, and penalties for violations.

## What Is an E-9 Visa Workplace Change?

The E-9 non-professional work visa operates through the Employment Permit System (EPS) administered by the [Ministry of Employment and Labor](https://www.moel.go.kr), assigning foreign workers to specific workplaces.
Foreign workers are, in principle, only permitted to work at the designated workplace upon entry.
However, if specific circumstances defined by law arise, the worker may change their workplace.

A workplace change is more than just switching jobs.
It requires two mandatory steps: obtaining a new employment permit from the Ministry of Employment and Labor AND filing a change of status notification with the immigration office.
Skipping either step results in illegal employment status.

## Permitted Reasons for Workplace Changes

Under Article 25 of the Act on Foreign Workers' Employment, workplace changes are permitted in the following circumstances.

**Employer-Attributed Reasons**

- The employer violated the terms of the employment contract
- There was workplace harassment, sexual harassment, or discrimination
- The business was suspended, closed, or the worker was laid off due to workforce restructuring
- The employer refused to renew the employment contract

**Reasons Not Attributable to the Foreign Worker**

- Unpaid wages due to employer bankruptcy or insolvency
- Inability to continue work at the current workplace due to industrial accident or illness
- Employer's refusal to renew the contract upon expiration

**Important:** Wanting better conditions or changing jobs for personal reasons is not permitted.
If the reason for change is not clearly substantiated, the employment center will reject the application.
Required documents differ by reason, so confirm in advance.

## Maximum Permitted Changes (3-Year Rule)

The number of workplace changes for E-9 visa holders is strictly limited.

| Period | Permitted Changes |
| --- | --- |
| Within the initial 3-year stay | Maximum 3 times |
| Within the re-entry period of 1 year and 10 months | Maximum 2 times |
| When employer-attributed reason occurs | Not counted toward the limit |

Exceeding 3 changes makes further changes impossible, and the worker must depart upon visa expiration.
However, changes caused by clear employer misconduct (assault, wage theft, etc.) are excluded from the count.
Your remaining permitted changes can be checked on the [EPS Portal](https://www.eps.go.kr).

## Ministry of Employment and Labor Permit Process

The first step in changing workplaces is obtaining approval from the Ministry of Employment and Labor (employment center).

**Step 1. Terminate Current Employment Contract**

Terminate the contract by mutual agreement with the employer and file an employment termination notification.
If the employer refuses to cooperate, you may directly report to the employment center and request ex officio processing.

**Step 2. Job Search**

Register as a job seeker at the employment center and search for new employers from the available job listing.
Use the [EPS Portal](https://www.eps.go.kr) job matching system.
The job search period is within 3 months from the date the change reason first arose.

**Step 3. New Workplace Employment Permit Application**

The new employer submits an employment permit application to the employment center.
The employment center reviews the workplace eligibility and issues a new employment permit.
Work at the new workplace may only begin after this step is completed.

## Immigration Status Change Notification

After obtaining the Ministry of Employment and Labor permit, the second step is filing a notification with the immigration office.

Within 14 days of receiving the new workplace employment permit, you must file a change of residence/status notification with the [Immigration and Foreign Affairs Office](https://www.immigration.go.kr).
Failing to file on time or at all results in illegal overstay status.

There are two filing methods:

- **In person**: Visit the competent immigration office or branch office
- **Online**: Use the [HI Korea](https://www.hikorea.go.kr) foreigner registration/notification menu

After filing, the workplace information on your Alien Registration Card (ARC) will be updated.

## Required Documents and Processing Times

### Documents Submitted to Ministry of Employment and Labor (Employer → Employment Center)

- Employment permit application form
- Copy of business registration certificate
- Proof of employment termination from previous workplace
- Supporting documents for the reason for change (varies by reason)
- New employment contract

### Documents Submitted to Immigration Office (Foreign Worker)

- Change of residence notification form
- Alien Registration Card (original)
- Copy of employment permit
- Copy of employment contract
- Copy of passport

**Processing Times**

| Agency | Processing Time |
| --- | --- |
| Employment center permit | 5–10 business days |
| Immigration office notification | Same day to 3 business days |

Processing times may vary depending on the workload at each employment center and immigration office.
During peak periods, delays may occur, so apply with adequate lead time.

## Penalties for Unauthorized Job Changes

Changing workplaces without Ministry of Employment and Labor approval results in the following consequences.

**For the Foreign Worker**

- Deportation upon detection of illegal employment
- Entry ban of 5 to 10 years
- Administrative fines
- Permanent bar from re-employment (blocked from EPS system registration)

**For the Employer (Hiring Without Permit)**

- Imprisonment of up to 3 years or fines of up to KRW 20 million
- Restriction from EPS participation for up to 3 years

The [Ministry of Employment and Labor](https://www.moel.go.kr) has recently intensified enforcement against illegal employment.
Deportation proceedings begin immediately upon detection, so make sure to follow the proper procedures.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1. What happens if I cannot find a new job after applying for a workplace change?**

If you cannot find a new workplace within 3 months of the change reason arising, you must depart Korea.
However, if there are unavoidable circumstances, you may apply to the immigration office for an extension of stay.
Handling varies by individual situation, so professional consultation is recommended.

**Q2. What can I do if my employer refuses to cooperate with terminating the contract?**

Even if your employer refuses to cooperate, you may apply directly to the employment center for a change.
If there are employer-attributed reasons such as wage theft or assault, bring supporting documents and request ex officio processing from the employment center.
Filing a labor complaint concurrently can speed up the process.

**Q3. Can I return to my original workplace after a change?**

In principle, yes, but you must go through a new employment permit process.
This also counts as one additional change.
In practice, the original employer must be willing to rehire you.

**Q4. I've used all 3 permitted changes. Can I change again?**

In principle, changes exceeding 3 times are not permitted.
However, if a clear employer-attributed reason occurs (assault, wage theft, business closure, etc.), an exception may be granted through a separate review.
In this case, you must prepare sufficient evidence and apply to the employment center.

**Q5. Am I allowed to work during the 3-month job search period?**

You may not legally engage in employment during the job search period.
However, you may begin working from the date the new employment permit is issued.
If the 3-month search period expires, you become subject to departure, so begin job searching promptly.
