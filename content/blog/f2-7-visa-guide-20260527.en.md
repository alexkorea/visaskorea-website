# F-2-7 Points-Based Residence Visa Korea 2026 — Complete Application Guide

The F-2-7 visa is Korea's points-based long-term residence visa for skilled foreign professionals.
If you score 80 points or more across criteria including age, education, Korean language ability, and income, you qualify to apply.
This guide covers the 2026 scoring table, eligibility requirements, and practical strategies to maximize your score.

## What is the F-2-7 Visa?

The F-2-7 is a sub-category of the F-2 (Residence) visa under Annex 1 of the Enforcement Decree of the Immigration Act.
It was designed to attract highly skilled foreign talent to Korea through an objective scoring system.

The key advantage of F-2-7 is **unrestricted employment** — unlike E-category work visas, there are no occupation code limitations. You can work in almost any legal profession.

## 2026 Scoring Criteria (Key Items)

A minimum total of **80 points** is required to apply.

**Age**

- 25–29: 25 points
- 30–34: 20 points
- 35–39: 15 points
- 40–44: 10 points
- 45+: 5 points

**Education**

- Korean STEM PhD: 40 points
- Korean STEM Master's: 35 points
- Korean STEM Bachelor's: 30 points
- Korean Humanities/Social Sciences PhD: 35 points
- Overseas PhD (QS top 200 university): 30 points

**Korean Language Ability (TOPIK)**

- TOPIK Level 5–6: 20 points
- TOPIK Level 4: 15 points
- TOPIK Level 3: 10 points
- TOPIK Level 2: 5 points

**Annual Income (based on GNI)**

- 3× GNI or more: 25 points
- 2–3× GNI: 20 points
- 1–2× GNI: 15 points
- Below GNI: 0 points

Note: Scoring criteria are updated periodically. Verify the latest table at [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr).

## Eligibility Requirements

- Currently holding a valid status of stay in Korea (E, D, or other categories)
- Total score of 80 points or more
- At least 1 year of lawful stay in Korea (exceptions may apply)
- No disqualifying factors (tax arrears, criminal history)

## Required Documents

- Status-of-stay change application form
- Passport and alien registration card (originals)
- Degree certificates with transcripts (apostille or notarized)
- Income certificate (withholding tax statement or equivalent)
- TOPIK score certificate (if applicable)
- Tax payment completion certificate
- Criminal background check (issued by Korean National Police Agency)

## Application Process

1. **Calculate your points** using the official scoring table
2. **Prepare documents** for all scoring categories you claim
3. **Submit application** at your regional immigration office
4. **Review period:** Approximately 3–6 weeks
5. **F-2-7 status granted:** Typically 2–3 year stay authorized upon approval

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1. Do overseas degrees count toward the education score?**

Yes, overseas degrees are accepted. Points vary depending on university ranking (e.g., QS World University Rankings).

**Q2. Is there any path to permanent residency from F-2-7?**

Yes. After sufficient stay on F-2-7, you may qualify for F-5 (permanent residence) if you meet the requirements.

**Q3. Can I work freely on F-2-7?**

Yes. F-2-7 allows you to work in virtually any legal occupation without occupation code restrictions.

**Q4. How long is the initial stay period?**

Typically 2–3 years, renewable while you maintain eligibility.
