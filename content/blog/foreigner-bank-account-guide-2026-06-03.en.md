## Basic Requirements

Foreigners with an Alien Registration Card (ARC) can open a bank account at Korean banks. Some banks accept foreigners on short-term visas with additional documentation.

## Documents by Visa Type

| Visa Type | Required Documents |
|-----------|--------------------|
| Long-term (D, E, F series) | ARC, passport, income proof (employed) |
| Overseas Korean (F-4) | ARC or domestic residence card, passport |
| Tourist (B-2, C-3) | Passport, visa, entry confirmation (varies by bank) |
| Permanent Resident (F-5) | ARC, passport |

## Non-Face-to-Face Account Opening

Several major banks (Shinhan SOL, Woori WON Banking, KakaoBank) now allow foreigners with ARC to open accounts via smartphone app without visiting a branch.

## Key Reminders

- Overseas remittances over USD 10,000 per transaction must be reported under Foreign Exchange Transaction Act
- Update your bank when your visa is renewed to prevent account suspension
