# D-10 Job Seeker Visa Korea 2026

The D-10 (Job Seeking) visa is issued to foreign nationals who wish to search for employment in Korea. It is primarily designed for recent graduates or returning professionals.

## Eligibility

| Category | Conditions |
|----------|-----------|
| Domestic university graduates | Bachelor's degree or above |
| Overseas university graduates | Bachelor's degree + intent to work in Korea |
| Returning professionals | Previous E-1~E-7 holders |

## Stay Period

- Initial: 6 months
- Extension: Up to 2 renewals (max 18 months total)

## Can You Work on D-10?

D-10 is **not** a work visa. Paid employment is not permitted. Once you receive a job offer, you must change status to E-7 or another appropriate work visa before starting work.

## Transitioning to E-7

1. Sign a formal employment contract with a Korean company
2. Obtain a ministry recommendation letter
3. Apply for status change from D-10 to E-7
4. Begin work after approval

## Required Documents

- Visa application form, passport
- Diploma or graduation certificate
- Job-seeking activity plan
