The F-2-5 visa is a residency status granted to foreigners who have legally resided in Korea for 5 or more years. Under **Immigration Control Act Enforcement Decree Schedule 1-2**, F-2 (residency) status is divided into several subtypes, with F-2-5 designated for long-term foreign residents.

This guide covers F-2-5 eligibility, required documents, application procedures, and how it compares with the F-2-7 points-based visa — updated for 2026.

## What is the F-2-5 Visa?

The F-2-5 visa is a residency status for foreigners who have completed 5 years of lawful stay in Korea.

Key features:
- **Stay duration:** Up to 5 years (renewable)
- **Activity scope:** Nearly all employment and business activities permitted
- **PR pathway:** F-2-5 holders can later apply for F-5 permanent residency
- **vs. F-2-7:** F-2-7 is points-based for skilled professionals; F-2-5 is stay duration-based

Holding F-2-5 status provides a level of residency stability comparable to permanent residency, and serves as the preparatory stage before applying for F-5.

## F-2-5 Visa Eligibility

**Core requirements:**
- 5 or more years of lawful stay in Korea (regardless of visa type)
- Clean criminal record (no criminal or immigration violations)
- Proof of financial self-sufficiency (income or assets)
- Basic Korean language ability (TOPIK Level 1 or completion of KIIP program)

**Bonus factors:**
- Korean language proficiency (TOPIK Level 3 or higher)
- Completion of Korea Immigration and Integration Program (KIIP)
- Volunteer activities, consistent tax payment record

Periods of unauthorized stay are excluded from the 5-year calculation. Consulting a professional to verify your eligible stay history is strongly recommended.

## Required Documents

- Status change application form
- Passport (valid for at least 6 months)
- Alien registration card
- Passport-size photo
- Documents showing 5+ years of stay history (passport visa pages, etc.)
- Income proof (withholding tax receipt, business income declaration)
- Asset proof (real estate registry, bank balance certificate)
- Criminal record check consent form
- Korean language proof (TOPIK score report or KIIP completion certificate)

Additional documents may be required depending on your current visa type.

## Application Procedure

1. **Make an appointment** at [HiKorea](https://www.hikorea.go.kr)
2. **Prepare and submit** required documents
3. **Await review** — comprehensive check of stay history, income, and legal compliance (typically 2–4 weeks)
4. **Receive F-2-5 status**
5. **Get updated alien registration card**

Ensure your contact information is clearly noted, as additional document requests may arise during review.

## F-2-5 vs F-2-7 — Which Visa Suits You?

| Criteria | F-2-5 | F-2-7 |
|---|---|---|
| Basis | 5+ years of stay | Points system (80+ points) |
| Target | Long-term foreign residents | Skilled professionals |
| Key factors | Stay history, income, Korean | Education, age, income, Korean, certs |
| Employment | Fully permitted | Fully permitted |
| F-5 pathway | Available | Available |

If you have 5+ years of stay but insufficient points, F-2-5 is the better path. If your stay is shorter but you meet the point threshold, F-2-7 is faster.

## From F-2-5 to F-5 Permanent Residency

After maintaining F-2-5 status for a qualifying period, you may apply for F-5 permanent residency.

- **Typical F-2-5 holding period before F-5 application:** 2+ years
- **F-5 requirements:** Stable income, financial self-sufficiency, no legal violations, good stay history
- **After F-5:** Permanent stay, unlimited employment and business activities

Optimal timing for F-5 application varies by individual stay history — early consultation is recommended.

## Key Cautions on Stay Calculation

The 5-year lawful stay requirement requires careful review:
- **Unauthorized stay periods excluded** from the 5-year count
- **Visa change gaps** during transitions can affect continuity
- **Extended overseas absence** (6+ months consecutive) may be excluded from stay calculation

Stay calculation standards have seen some updates in 2026. Professional verification of your total qualifying stay is advised.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1. Does student visa (D-2) time count toward the 5 years?**

Yes, D-2 student visa periods count toward the 5-year lawful stay calculation, provided there were no unauthorized stay periods.

**Q2. Can F-2-5 holders work in any field?**

Employment activities are broadly permitted. Exceptions include occupations where foreign employment is restricted by specific laws.

**Q3. Do I need Korean language ability to apply?**

TOPIK Level 1 or completion of a qualifying KIIP stage is a basic requirement. Insufficient Korean ability may make the application more difficult.

**Q4. Can I apply for F-2-5 and F-2-7 simultaneously?**

Only one residency status can be held at a time. Consult a specialist to determine which pathway is better for your situation.

**Q5. How long does it take to transition from F-2-5 to F-5?**

This depends on your stay history and whether requirements are met. Generally, applying after 2+ years of F-2-5 is advisable.
