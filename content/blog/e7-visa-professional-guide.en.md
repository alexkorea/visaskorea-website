# E-7 Specialized Occupation Visa Korea 2026

The E-7 (Specific Activities) visa is issued to foreign nationals who will work in Korea in government-designated specialized occupations requiring professional skills or knowledge.

## Eligible Occupations

| Field | Examples |
|-------|---------|
| IT / Software | Systems analysts, software developers, AI researchers |
| Finance | Financial analysts, international accountants |
| Design | Industrial designers, UX/UI designers |
| Maritime / Aviation | Ship captains, commercial pilots |

## Key Requirements

- **Employment contract** with a Korean company
- **Education**: Bachelor's degree or above in a relevant field
- **Ministry recommendation letter**: Required from the relevant government ministry
- **Salary**: Generally at or above the per-capita GNI level

## Recommendation Letter by Field

| Field | Issuing Ministry |
|-------|----------------|
| IT / Software | Ministry of Science and ICT |
| Finance | Financial Services Commission |
| Culture / Arts | Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism |

The recommendation letter is often the longest part of the process. Contact the relevant ministry early.

## Required Documents

- Visa application form, passport, photo
- Employment contract (Korean/English)
- Ministry recommendation letter
- Diploma and career certificate
- Company business registration

## Processing Time and Stay Period

- Processing: 5–10 business days
- Initial stay: 1–3 years, renewable
