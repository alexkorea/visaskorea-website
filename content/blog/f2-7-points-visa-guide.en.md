# F-2-7 Points-Based Long-Term Residency Visa Guide Korea 2026

The F-2-7 (Residency – Points System) visa allows skilled foreign nationals to obtain long-term residency in Korea by scoring 80 or more out of 120 across categories including education, income, age, and Korean language proficiency.

---

## 1. What Is the F-2-7 Visa?

The F-2-7 is a points-based residency visa for professional foreigners. It comes with no employment restrictions and allows stays of up to 3 years, renewable.

- **Eligible:** Foreigners legally residing in Korea with professional qualifications
- **Stay duration:** Initial 1–3 years (renewable)
- **Work rights:** Unrestricted — all occupations allowed
- **Family:** Spouse and minor children eligible for F-3 dependent visa

---

## 2. Points Scoring System (80+ out of 120 required)

| Category | Max Points |
|----------|-----------|
| Age (peak: 25–29) | 20 |
| Education (STEM Master's or higher preferred) | 35 |
| Income (relative to GNI) | 20 |
| Korean language (TOPIK) | 10 |
| Domestic work/residence history | 15 |
| Bonus (certifications, investment, volunteering) | +10 |
| Deductions (immigration violations, etc.) | up to −20 |

**You need 80+ points to apply.** Calculate your score before gathering documents.

---

## 3. Eligibility Requirements

- **Lawful status:** Valid Korean visa/ARC
- **Income:** Annual income ≥ GNI (~₩42M/year)
- **Residence history:** 1+ year of domestic stay
- **Clean record:** No criminal history

## 5. Application Process

1. **Calculate your points** — Use the immigration office scoring sheet or consult a scrivener
2. **Gather documents** — Education, income, and language proofs
3. **Submit application** — In person at local immigration office or via HiKorea online
4. **Wait for review** — Typically 2–4 weeks
5. **After F-2-7 issuance** — Begin unrestricted long-term stay

---

## 6. F-2-7 to F-5 (Permanent Residency)

After holding F-2-7 for 3+ years, you become eligible to apply for F-5 permanent residency.

- **Minimum stay:** 3 years under F-2-7
- **Income:** Maintained at GNI or above
- **Tax compliance:** No arrears
- **No violations**

F-5 holders do not need to renew their residency status — it is permanent.

---

## 7. FAQ

**Q. I have an E-7 visa. Can I apply for F-2-7?**
A. Yes. Any foreigner on a valid status who meets the point threshold can apply for a status change.

**Q. How do I calculate my score?**
A. Use the official scoring sheet from the immigration office website, or consult an immigration scrivener for an accurate count.

**Q. Can I apply without any Korean language skills?**
A. Yes. You'll receive 0 points for the TOPIK category, but if you reach 80 points through other categories, you can still apply.
