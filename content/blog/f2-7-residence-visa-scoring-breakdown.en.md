# F-2 Residence Visa Points System: How Each Category Is Scored and Where Results Diverge

The F-2-7 points system comes down to whether you can reliably secure 60 points or more from the base categories out of a total of 80.

Eligible applicants include long-term holders of E-1–E-7, D-5–D-10, and certain residence-status visas. Education, income, age, and Korean language ability form the backbone of the base score.

This article walks through the scoring structure by category, bonus and deduction items, the points where applicants frequently get stuck during review, and the details most often overlooked when calculating scores.

<h2 id="section-1">Basic Structure of the F-2 Residence Visa Points System</h2>

F-2-7 scores are calculated by adding base category points to bonus and deduction items.

The standard threshold is typically 80 points, and your score must clear this line for the application to be meaningful in the first place.

The governing regulations are outlined in the [Korea Immigration Service HiKorea](https://www.hikorea.go.kr) guide and the appendices of the [Enforcement Decree of the Immigration Control Act](https://www.law.go.kr).

### The Five Base Categories

The base categories are education, Korean language ability, age, income, and length of stay in Korea.

These five account for more than 70% of the total score.

In practice, education and income are where scores diverge the most.

### Bonus and Deduction Items

Bonus points are awarded for completing the Korea Immigration and Integration Program (KIIP), volunteer work, domestic certifications, and graduation from top-tier universities.

Deductions arise from Immigration Control Act violations, tax delinquency, and criminal records.

A commonly missed point is that even past minor fines or simple violations can count as deductions.

<h2 id="section-2">How Education Points Are Calculated</h2>

Education is scored based solely on your highest qualification — only one tier counts.

Points decrease in order from doctorate to master's, bachelor's, and associate degree.

What matters more is whether the degree was earned in Korea or abroad, which determines additional bonus points.

### Bonus for Domestic Degrees

Degrees earned at Korean universities carry a separate bonus.

In particular, degrees from top-ranked Korean universities receive additional bonus points.

That said, the list of universities eligible for the bonus is adjusted each year, so you must verify whether your alma mater currently qualifies right before applying.

### Recognition Range for Overseas Degrees

For overseas degrees, if degree verification and consular authentication are missing, the points are often counted as zero altogether.

Even if you have all the documents, missing the authentication step will stop you cold.

If this part is weak, your entire education score disappears.

<h2 id="section-3">How Income Points Are Calculated</h2>

Income is assessed based on your annual income from the most recent year or the previous tax year.

The threshold amount is tied to GNI (Gross National Income) and changes each year.

In practice, the consistency of your income documentation matters even more.

### Income Documentation Flow

Your withholding tax receipt for earned income, income certificate, and four major insurance enrollment records all need to align.

| Document | Issuing Authority | Verification Point |
|------|--------|-------------|
| Income Certificate | [National Tax Service Hometax](https://www.hometax.go.kr) | Based on prior tax year |
| Withholding Tax Receipt | Your employer | Company seal/signature |
| Four Major Insurance Records | National Health Insurance Service | Any gaps in enrollment |
| Employment Certificate | Your employer | Position and work period |

When there are amount discrepancies between the three documents, reviewers often take the lowest figure as the baseline.

### Self-Employed and Business Income Earners

For business income earners, an income certificate alone is often not enough.

VAT filings, business premises lease agreements, and even client records may be reviewed, so the actual flow of revenue needs to be visible.

Even with high income, if the flow isn't clearly explained, getting the points recognized becomes difficult.

<h2 id="section-4">Age and Length of Stay Points</h2>

Age scoring peaks for those in their late 20s to late 30s.

Points decrease in stages from age 40 onward.

Length of stay is calculated based on cumulative legal residence.

### Cautions When Calculating Length of Stay

Length of stay isn't a simple accumulation of entry dates — each visa status has its own recognition range.

| Visa Status | Recognition Range | Notes |
|-----------|-----------|------|
| D-2 Student | Partial | Status-specific ratio applied |
| D-10 Job Seeker | Limited | Short-term status |
| E-1–E-7 Employment | Full | Legal work period |
| F-4 Overseas Korean | Recognized | Status-specific calculation |

> **Note:** If there are gaps between departure and re-entry, those periods are excluded. If you've frequently come and gone on short-term stays, the cumulative calculation may work against you.

If your residence status has changed year over year, you'll need a case-by-case review to determine the exact recognized period.

<h2 id="section-5">Korean Language Ability and Social Integration Bonuses</h2>

Korean language ability is proven through TOPIK level or completion of stages in the Korea Immigration and Integration Program (KIIP).

Even at the same level, additional bonus points differ depending on which route you used.

### TOPIK vs. KIIP

TOPIK has clear scoring by level, but the bonus margin is limited.

Completing Stage 5 of the [Korea Immigration and Integration Program](https://www.socinet.go.kr) earns both Korean language category points and additional bonus points, so in practice KIIP offers better point efficiency.

In particular, passing the KIIP comprehensive evaluation lets you secure both Korean language and social integration bonuses at the same time.

### Volunteer Work and Certification Bonuses

Bonus points are awarded once Ministry of Justice-certified volunteer hours pass a certain cumulative threshold.

Losing your volunteer hour certificate is common, but recognition requires official records remaining on platforms such as the 1365 Volunteer Portal.

Domestic national technical qualifications are scored separately by grade level.

<h2 id="section-6">Deduction Items and Practical Pitfalls</h2>

Because deductions are subtracted from the total, even strong scores in other categories can collapse in one stroke.

### History of Immigration Law Violations

The most common deduction triggers are past overstays, unreported activities outside your visa status, and failure to report changes in workplace.

Even minor disposition records you've forgotten about will surface during review.

If any of this is processed as undeclared, the application itself may be rejected.

### Tax and Fine Delinquency

Unpaid national taxes, local taxes, or fines can not only trigger deductions but also block you at the application stage.

Before applying, you must check your delinquency records in advance through [Gov24](https://www.gov.kr).

| Deduction Item | Verification Route | Direction for Handling |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| Immigration Law Violation | Immigration record inquiry | Prepare advance explanation materials |
| National Tax Delinquency | Hometax | Attach proof of full payment |
| Local Tax Delinquency | Wetax | Attach proof of full payment |
| Criminal Record | Criminal record certificate | Case-by-case review |

Recent adjustments have been made to how some deduction items are applied, so you'll need to check with the relevant authority on how your specific history is currently treated.

<h2 id="section-7">Commonly Overlooked Points in Score Calculation</h2>

Even with abundant documentation, results can vary if the scoring logic doesn't line up.

### Items Cannot Be Double-Counted

Only one degree — your highest — counts under the education category.

Some applicants try to combine points from a master's and a bachelor's degree, but this kind of stacking is not recognized.

### Bonuses Don't Accumulate Indefinitely

Each bonus category has a cap, so no matter how many volunteer hours you log, they only count up to a fixed limit.

Getting fixated on bonuses while neglecting base category reinforcement is the most regrettable pattern seen in practice.

### Scoring as of the Application Date

Points are calculated as of the application submission date.

If a certification result announcement or the validity period of your TOPIK score expires just before you apply, it won't count — so timing your schedule is critical.

<h2 id="section-8">FAQ</h2>

**Q1. Is 80 points the correct passing threshold for the F-2-7 points system?**

The base threshold is 80 points, but actual passing lines vary case by case depending on bonuses and deductions. You'll need to reconfirm your score calculation right before applying.

**Q2. Is reaching 80 points possible with only a bachelor's degree?**

Yes. But you'll need to make up the shortfall in education through income, Korean language ability, age, and length of stay. Completing KIIP Stage 5 is especially a decisive reinforcement card.

**Q3. Is only income in the applicant's own name recognized?**

Generally, only the applicant's own employment or business income is counted. A spouse's income does not factor into the score calculation.

**Q4. Does time on a D-10 job-seeking visa count toward length of stay?**

D-10 is a short-term status, so its recognition rate is limited. Because each status has different recognition ranges, your full residence history needs to be considered for accuracy.

**Q5. Does a single past disposition record significantly affect the score?**

The deduction margin depends on the severity of the case. Simple reporting omissions and detected out-of-status activities have different application criteria, so case-by-case verification is needed.

**Q6. If F-2-7 points are insufficient, what's the fastest way to reinforce them?**

Completing KIIP stages and obtaining domestic national technical qualifications are the fastest bonus routes. That said, you first need to accurately identify where your base category weaknesses lie.
