# E-7 Special Activities Visa for Foreign Professionals in Korea 2026

## What is the E-7 Visa?

The E-7 (Special Activities) visa is issued to foreign nationals who will engage in specific professional activities designated by the Minister of Justice. It covers IT, engineering, construction, manufacturing, healthcare, and many other professional sectors.

## Eligibility Requirements

### Occupation Requirements
The position must be listed in the Ministry of Justice's E-7 permitted occupation list. Requirements vary by occupation category.

### Education and Experience
- Bachelor's degree or higher in the relevant field, or
- Minimum 5 years of practical experience in the relevant field
- Some occupations require national technical qualifications

### Employment Requirements
- Must have a confirmed job offer from a Korean company
- Some occupations require an employment permit issued in advance

## Application Process

1. **Secure employment**: Sign a standard employment contract
2. **Employer applies for employment permit** (if required)
3. **Visa application**: At overseas Korean embassy or domestic immigration office
4. **Processing time**: Typically 2–8 weeks
