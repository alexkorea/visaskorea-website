## What is the E-9 Visa?

The E-9 (Non-Professional Employment) visa is issued under Korea's Employment Permit System (EPS) to foreign nationals from 16 designated countries who work in manufacturing, construction, agriculture, fisheries, and select service industries.

## Eligible Industries

- Manufacturing: businesses with fewer than 300 employees or capital below KRW 8 billion
- Construction, agriculture, livestock, fisheries
- Designated service sectors (food service, recycling, etc.)

## Application Process

1. Employer files domestic job posting at Employment Center
2. If no domestic hire, employer applies for Employment Permit
3. Foreign workers selected through Korea Human Resources Development Service (HRD Korea)
4. Visa issuance confirmation → applicant applies at Korean embassy abroad
5. Entry → employment training → health screening → alien registration

## Length of Stay

- Initial: 3 years
- Extension: 1 year 10 months (total 4 years 10 months)
- Re-entry: up to 4 years 10 months additional
