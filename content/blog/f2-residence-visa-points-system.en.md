<h1>F-2 Residence Visa Points System Complete Guide</h1>

The F-2 residence visa points system is a program that supports foreigners to reside stably in Korea. To obtain this visa, various points must be accumulated to reach a certain standard, based on individual qualification requirements.


<div>### Key Summary
The F-2 visa allows foreigners to reside in Korea.

## Scoring Criteria
The points for the F-2 visa are calculated based on the following items:


Age: Individuals aged 18 to 55 receive points.
Education: A minimum of a high school diploma is required, and points vary based on the degree.
Work Experience: Additional points can be earned based on work experience.
Korean Language Proficiency: Points are awarded based on TOPIK scores.


The scoring criteria need to be confirmed with the relevant authorities, and a score of 80 or above is required to apply for the F-2 visa.

## Application Process
To apply for the F-2 visa, the following steps must be followed:

1. Prepare the necessary documents
2. Submit the application form to the relevant immigration office
3. The review period generally takes 2-4 weeks
4. After visa issuance, registration is required according to the duration of stay

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: How is the F-2 visa score calculated?
A: It is calculated based on age, education, work experience, and Korean language proficiency.

### Q: How long is the F-2 visa valid?
A: It is generally valid for 2 years and can be renewed.

### Q: What if my score is insufficient?
A: You need to meet additional qualification requirements or find ways to increase your score.

### Q: What if my Korean language proficiency is lacking?
A: You can obtain a score by certifying your Korean language proficiency through the TOPIK exam.

### Q: What should I do if my visa application is denied?
A: Check the reasons for denial and take the necessary actions before reapplying.

### Q: What is the difference between the F-2 visa and the F-5 visa?
A: The F-5 visa is for permanent residency and provides a more stable residency status than the F-2 visa.

### Q: How long does it take to process a visa application?
A: It generally takes 2-4 weeks, but it may vary depending on the relevant authorities.
