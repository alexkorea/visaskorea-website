<h2 id="scoring">Scoring Criteria</h2>
The points for the F-2 visa are calculated based on the following items:

<ul>
<li>Age: Individuals aged 18 to 55 receive points.</li>
<li>Education: A minimum of a high school diploma is required, and points vary based on the degree.</li>
<li>Work Experience: Additional points can be earned based on work experience.</li>
<li>Korean Language Proficiency: Points are awarded based on TOPIK scores.</li>
</ul>

The scoring criteria need to be confirmed with the relevant authorities, and a score of 80 or above is required to apply for the F-2 visa.

<h2 id="application">Application Process</h2>
To apply for the F-2 visa, the following steps must be followed:

1. Prepare the necessary documents
2. Submit the application form to the relevant immigration office
3. The review period generally takes 2-4 weeks
4. After visa issuance, registration is required according to the duration of stay

<h2 id="faq">Frequently Asked Questions</h2>

<h3>Q: How is the F-2 visa score calculated?</h3>
A: It is calculated based on age, education, work experience, and Korean language proficiency.

<h3>Q: How long is the F-2 visa valid?</h3>
A: It is generally valid for 2 years and can be renewed.

<h3>Q: What if my score is insufficient?</h3>
A: You need to meet additional qualification requirements or find ways to increase your score.

<h3>Q: What if my Korean language proficiency is lacking?</h3>
A: You can obtain a score by certifying your Korean language proficiency through the TOPIK exam.

<h3>Q: What should I do if my visa application is denied?</h3>
A: Check the reasons for denial and take the necessary actions before reapplying.

<h3>Q: What is the difference between the F-2 visa and the F-5 visa?</h3>
A: The F-5 visa is for permanent residency and provides a more stable residency status than the F-2 visa.

<h3>Q: How long does it take to process a visa application?</h3>
A: It generally takes 2-4 weeks, but it may vary depending on the relevant authorities.</h3>
