## Overview of F-5 Permanent Residency

Korea's F-5 visa grants **permanent, unrestricted residency** — no renewal required, no employment restrictions. It is the most stable long-term status available to foreign nationals in Korea.

## Main Pathways to F-5

### 1. F-2-7 Points-Based Route
Accumulate 80+ points under the F-2-7 scoring system and reside legally for 5+ years. This is the most common pathway for general foreign residents.

### 2. Marriage Immigration (F-6 to F-5)
Married to a Korean national? After 2 years of cohabitation in Korea on an F-6 visa, you may apply for F-5. Basic Korean language ability is required.

### 3. Professional Worker (E-7 to F-5)
Skilled professionals in IT, engineering, medicine, or finance can apply after 5 years on an E-7 visa with income meeting or exceeding the national per-capita GNI.

### 4. Investment Immigration
Invest KRW 1.5 billion or more in a government-approved fund or designated real estate area. No Korean language or age requirements. After 3 years of residence, F-5 is available.

### 5. Advanced Technology Talent
Recognized specialists in AI, semiconductors, bio, or other designated fields receive expedited processing and relaxed requirements.
