## What to Do When You Lose Your ARC

If you lose your Alien Registration Card (ARC), you must apply for a replacement within **14 days** under Article 36 of the Immigration Act. The lost card report and replacement application are processed together.

## Where to Apply

- Local immigration office or branch
- Some areas: community center (via Government24 proxy service)

## Required Documents

1. ARC replacement application form
2. Original passport
3. 1 ID photo
4. Statement of loss (free format, explaining circumstances)
5. Fee: KRW 30,000

## Processing Time

- Immediate issuance on-site or **3–5 business days** by mail

## Proving Your Identity Before Replacement

Before your new ARC arrives, you can prove your identity using your passport plus an immigration status confirmation letter (issued at the immigration office).

## FAQ

**Q. Can I leave Korea without my ARC?**
A. You can depart using your passport, but re-entry issues may arise. We recommend applying for a replacement before travel.

**Q. Is online application available?**
A. ARC replacement currently requires an in-person visit.
