# E-2 Conversation Instructor Visa Eligibility Guide — Education, Experience & Criminal Record Standards

The E-2 conversation instructor visa requires three conditions to be met simultaneously before review even begins: **a bachelor's degree or higher, citizenship in a designated English-speaking country, and a criminal background check certificate**.

It is open to foreign nationals from the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Ireland, Australia, New Zealand, and South Africa who hold at least a bachelor's degree.

This guide walks through the scope of accepted academic credentials, criminal record certification procedures, employer requirements, and denial grounds — all from a practitioner's perspective.

<h2 id="section-1">Core Eligibility Requirements for the E-2 Visa</h2>

### Nationality — Only 7 Countries Are Recognized

The E-2 conversation instructor (English) visa is restricted to nationals of seven countries where English is the native language.

These are the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Ireland, Australia, New Zealand, and South Africa.

Dual nationals sometimes run into branching outcomes depending on which passport they choose, so confirmation in advance is essential.

Conversation instruction in other languages (Chinese, Japanese, French, etc.) follows separate standards with a different nationality scope.

> **Note:** Holding only permanent residency without citizenship in one of these seven countries does not qualify you for the E-2 conversation instructor (English) visa.

### Academic Credentials — A Bachelor's Degree Is the Baseline

A bachelor's degree or higher is required to apply.

The major is not restricted, but the degree **must be a regular four-year bachelor's degree**, and online degrees face strict scrutiny.

South African applicants may additionally need to prove that part or all of their primary or secondary education was completed in an English-speaking country.

Whether your degree qualifies depends on your university and program, so verifying eligibility in advance is wise.

### Criminal Background Check — The Most Common Stumbling Block

A criminal background certificate issued by your home country is mandatory.

For the U.S., the FBI-issued version is the standard; for Canada, the RCMP-issued version.

After issuance, it must go through apostille or consular authentication, and certificates issued too long ago will not be accepted.

In practice, the most common pitfall is sequencing the issuance date and apostille processing incorrectly.

> **Practical Tip:** Criminal background certificates are typically expected to be issued within six months of entry, but recognition periods can shift with immigration policy changes — check with the relevant authority right before issuance.

<h2 id="section-2">Scope of Recognized Education and Experience for E-2</h2>

### Degree Recognition — Determined by School and Program

While the bachelor's degree itself does not require a specific major, the regularity of the institution and the verifiability of the degree are reviewed first.

Distance learning degrees, diploma programs, and language course completion certificates do not qualify as bachelor's degrees.

For South African nationals, confirmation of where secondary education was completed serves alongside the degree as evidence of an English-speaking environment.

| Item | Recognition Status | Notes |
|------|-------------------|-------|
| 4-year regular bachelor's | Recognized | Any major |
| 2-year Associate degree | Not recognized | Additional bachelor's required |
| Online degree | Difficult to recognize | Regularity checked separately |
| Honorary degree | Not recognized | Not a regular degree |
| Only master's/doctorate held | Case-by-case | Separate bachelor's proof needed |

### Experience Requirements — Usually Substituted by Education

The E-2 conversation instructor (English) visa does not mandate prior English teaching experience.

That said, employers (language academies, university-affiliated language institutes, etc.) often impose their own experience or certification requirements (TESOL, TEFL, CELTA, etc.) during hiring.

In practice, even applicants with no experience can obtain an E-2 as long as the bachelor's degree, qualifying nationality, and clean criminal record requirements are met.

### Background Check and Health Screening

After issuance, the standard flow at the foreigner registration stage involves a background check and submission of health screening results covering items such as narcotics and communicable diseases.

Screening items shift with immigration policy revisions, so whether a specific item applies to your case is best confirmed by a professional.

<h2 id="section-3">Employer Requirements for the E-2 Visa</h2>

### Eligible Employer Categories

| Institution Type | E-2 Hiring Allowed | Notes |
|------------------|-------------------|-------|
| Registered language academy | Yes | Academy registration certificate required |
| Elementary/middle/high school | Yes | Education office hiring or contracted |
| University-affiliated language institute | Yes | Must be a formal affiliate |
| Broadcast language programs | Yes | Stricter separate review |
| Unregistered study rooms / private tutoring | No | Strong grounds for denial |
| In-house corporate English classes | No | Outside E-2 scope |

### Key Points in the Employment Contract

Work location, weekly teaching hours, compensation, and contract term must all be clearly stated.

If work spans multiple locations, the scope of activity at branches beyond the main site requires additional verification.

Contracts that allocate a heavy share of duties to non-teaching work (administration, planning, etc.) may be viewed as falling outside the conversation instructor scope, and insufficient explanation here triggers requests for supplementary documents.

### The Institution's Administrative Standing

Institutions that have been recently established or carry recent disciplinary records are weighed less favorably in hiring credibility assessments.

What matters more than the outward appearance of the institution is its registration status and history of administrative actions.

<h2 id="section-4">Documents Required for the E-2 Visa Application</h2>

### Documents the Applicant Must Prepare

- Copy of passport
- Photos (in proper specification)
- Bachelor's degree certificate (apostille or consular authentication)
- Academic credential verification (if needed)
- Criminal background certificate (apostille or consular authentication)
- Personal statement or résumé
- Health screening certificate (at foreigner registration stage)

### Documents the Employer Must Prepare

- Copy of business registration certificate
- Academy registration certificate or school establishment authorization
- Employment contract
- Recruitment justification statement
- Teaching schedule or instructor assignment chart
- Tax-related supporting documents

### Frequently Missed Documents

Credential verification, apostille processing, and the teaching schedule are the most commonly overlooked items.

Even with many documents on hand, if the two pillars — degree and criminal record — are weak, supplementary requests drag on.

<h2 id="section-5">Grounds for E-2 Visa Denial and Cautions</h2>

### Commonly Cited Denial Grounds

- Degree cannot be verified, or forgery/alteration is suspected
- Criminal background certificate issued too long ago, or missing apostille
- Employer's academy registration deficiency or administrative penalty history
- Applicant's past immigration record showing overstay or forced departure
- Contract showing duties other than the visa's permitted scope as the main work

### Considerations When Reapplying After a Denial

A case that has already been denied rarely flips on simple resubmission.

Start by identifying exactly where the denial originated, then assess whether the same issue can be remedied.

> **Note:** When the denial notice is abstract or only states something like "insufficient qualifications," determining which item actually fell short during review requires further analysis.

### Recent Changes

Standards for E-2 criminal record acceptance and academic credential verification procedures are periodically updated.

To confirm the latest standards applicable to your case, it is safest to consult the [Korea Law Information Center](https://www.law.go.kr) for the Immigration Act enforcement decree alongside notices from your local immigration office.

<h2 id="section-6">Managing Your Status After E-2 Issuance</h2>

### Foreigner Registration and Residence

Foreigner registration must be completed within 90 days of entry.

Changes of address and changes of workplace carry reporting obligations, and missing them can result in fines or become a sticking point during stay extension.

### Changing Workplaces and Adding Teaching Engagements

Under E-2, teaching outside the registered employer is generally restricted.

Teaching at another academy or signing a separate teaching contract may constitute an out-of-scope activity requiring prior authorization.

Processing times vary by immigration office, and we identify the fastest route for your case.

### Stay Extension

At the extension stage, teaching performance, tax records, and the employer's status are all evaluated together.

If these areas are weak, the supplementary requests at extension can become more demanding than at the initial application.

<h2 id="section-7">Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)</h2>

**Q1. I'm a native English speaker but not a citizen of one of the 7 countries — can I apply for E-2?**

In principle, no.

The E-2 conversation instructor (English) visa is gated on nationality, and an English-speaking background alone does not substitute for it.

**Q2. When should I get my criminal background certificate issued?**

Right before applying is safest.

If the issuance date is too old or the apostille sequence is wrong, you'll frequently need to obtain it again.

**Q3. Can I qualify for E-2 with an online bachelor's degree?**

It's difficult.

The regularity of the degree and the institution's accreditation are scrutinized further, and there is a high chance of falling outside the recognized scope.

**Q4. Can I work at an in-house corporate English class instead of a language academy?**

That falls outside the E-2 scope of activity.

That arrangement requires a different visa status or separate authorization review.

**Q5. How much does it cost?**

Costs vary by case, so we provide precise figures during the free consultation.

The basic structure is government-published fees plus administrative processing costs.

**Q6. If I've been denied before, is it hard to get approved later?**

It depends on the denial reason.

Remedying formal requirements like degree and criminal record is feasible, but suspicion of forgery or alteration becomes very tough to overcome.
