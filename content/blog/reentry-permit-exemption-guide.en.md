<h1>Foreign Re-entry Permit Exemption System Guide</h1>

The foreign re-entry permit exemption system allows foreigners to return to Korea without obtaining a re-entry permit if they meet certain conditions while residing in Korea. This system has been established to assist foreign residents and applies only when specific conditions are met.


<div>### Key Summary
The foreign re-entry permit exemption applies to foreigners who meet specific conditions.
It is important to accurately understand the application method and required documents.
The processing period typically takes 1-2 weeks.
</div>

## Eligibility for Re-entry Permit Exemption
The following individuals are eligible for the re-entry permit exemption system:
- Foreigners residing in Korea who are in the process of visa extension or change
- Individuals holding a foreign registration card
- Foreigners from specific countries that have signed a visa waiver agreement with Korea

## Application Method
To receive the re-entry permit exemption, the following procedures must be followed:
1. Apply online or through the relevant immigration office.
2. Fill out and submit the application form.

## Processing Procedure
The processing procedure after application is as follows:
1. Notification of results within 1-2 weeks after document submission.
2. Re-entry is possible once approved.

## Common Mistakes
<div>⚠️ <strong>Note:</strong> Missing application forms and incorrect document submissions are common. It is important to prepare all documents accurately.</div>

### Q: What is the minimum residency period required to apply for the re-entry permit exemption?
### A: Generally, a specific period of residency is required, but the exact conditions need to be confirmed with the relevant authorities.

### Q: When can I expect to receive the results after applying for the re-entry permit exemption?
### A: Results are typically notified within 1-2 weeks.

### Q: Is it possible to apply for the re-entry permit exemption if my foreign registration card has expired?
### A: Applications cannot be made with an expired foreign registration card; it must be renewed first.

### Q: Can I receive the re-entry permit exemption if I need a visa extension?
### A: If a visa extension is needed, the relevant conditions must be met to receive the re-entry permit exemption.

### Q: What is the fee for applying for the re-entry permit exemption?
### A: Generally, there is no fee, but costs may arise for issuing specific documents.

### Q: What is the validity period of the re-entry permit exemption?
### A: The validity period varies depending on the approval date and may differ by case.

### Q: What should I do if changes occur after applying for the re-entry permit exemption?
### A: If changes occur, you must contact the relevant immigration office immediately.
