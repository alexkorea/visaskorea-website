# F-5 Permanent Residency in Korea 2026

The F-5 (Permanent Resident) status is granted to foreigners who meet the requirements set forth in the Enforcement Decree of the Immigration Act.

## Main Application Types

### Type 1 — From F-2 (Resident) to F-5
- Must have held F-2-7 residency for at least 2 years
- Income requirement: at least 60% of per capita GNI of the previous year

### Type 2 — 10+ Years of Lawful Stay
- Continuous lawful residence for 10+ years (includes marriage migration)
- Income requirement applies

### Type 3 — Elderly Parent Invitation
- Parent of a Korean national or permanent resident (age 60+)
- Child must provide financial guarantee

## Required Documents

1. Integrated application form
2. Passport and alien registration card
3. Income verification (withholding tax certificate or tax return)
4. Residence confirmation
5. Tax payment certificate

## Processing Time

3 to 6 months after submission. Current visa status must be maintained throughout.
