# F-2-7 Points-Based Permanent Residency in Korea 2026

The F-2-7 visa allows foreigners legally residing in Korea to obtain long-term residency based on a point evaluation system managed by the Ministry of Justice.

## Basic Requirements

- Currently in legal status (no overstay history)
- At least 1 year of residence in Korea over the past 5 years
- **80 or more points** (out of 200)

## Points Summary

| Category | Max Points |
|---|---|
| Age (25–29 scores highest) | 20 |
| Education (PhD scores highest) | 35 |
| Korean language (TOPIK) | 20 |
| Income (vs GNI benchmark) | 40 |
| Bonus (investment, employment, etc.) | 30 |

## Common Bottlenecks

Income verification is the most frequent obstacle. Employees submit the withholding tax certificate; self-employed applicants often need to provide an additional comprehensive income tax payment confirmation.

## Required Documents

1. Integrated application form (immigration office format)
2. Passport and alien registration card
3. Score verification documents (diploma, TOPIK certificate, income proof)
4. Criminal background check consent
5. TB screening result (nationals of designated countries)
